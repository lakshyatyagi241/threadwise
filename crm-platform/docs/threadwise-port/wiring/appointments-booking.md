[inventory/appointments-booking.md](../inventory/appointments-booking.md); the API is [docs/api/appointments-booking.md](../../api/appointments-booking.md). This file is the record of who feeds

## BL-281 rows (bookings in the contact's Meetings)

A booking that has a contact is also a row of `contact_meetings` (`source = 'booking'`, `appointment_id`, `join_url`), so the contact's Meetings list shows every occasion with that person. The Appointments section of the contact stays as it is. The appointments module imports the crm module (never the reverse): the mirror is written through crm's exported `ContactMeetingsRepository`, and the crm side reads the appointment's id, status and people by joining `appointments` in its list query.

| Id            | What                                                                                                                           | Fed by                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Read by                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WIRE-APPT-008 | The mirror row (`AppointmentMeetingMirror.sync`), one per appointment, unique on (org_id, appointment_id)                      | `BookingService.book` / `reschedule` / `transition`, `AppointmentsService.update` (title, place, contact) and `remove` (`forget`), `AppointmentCalendarWriter` (the Meet or Teams link), all in the appointment write transaction | `GET /v2/contacts/:id/meetings`; the contact page Meetings tab, quick view, Overview summary strip and the Activity timeline                                                          |
| WIRE-APPT-009 | The status of a mirrored row is never stored: the list joins the appointment                                                   | `appointments.status`                                                                                                                                                                                                             | the list (`status`, the `upcoming` / `past` filter), the web chips and the "still to come" counts (cancelled and declined are never upcoming)                                         |
| WIRE-APPT-010 | `canManageBooking` per row                                                                                                     | the caller's `record.appointments.update` grant and its record scope against the appointment's host and booker (`own`, `team`, `all`)                                                                                             | the web (View booking or Manage booking); `PATCH` and `DELETE` of a linked booked row are refused with `409 CONFLICT` for everyone (it is changed on the appointment)                 |
| WIRE-APPT-011 | Back-fill of existing bookings (queue `meeting-mirror-backfill`, `MeetingMirrorBackfillScheduler` at worker start)             | every non-cancelled, non-declined appointment with a contact and no row yet; privileged, 500 per transaction, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`                                                                                            | `contact_meetings` (no audit and no events for a back-fill row); a repeat run writes nothing                                                                                          |
| WIRE-APPT-012 | Outbox `contact_meeting.created`, `.updated`, `.deleted` from the mirror, payload `{ uuid, source: "booking", appointmentId }` | the mirror; audit module `contact_meetings`                                                                                                                                                                                       | nothing subscribes to `contact_meeting.*` today. A future consumer must skip `source: "booking"`: the same booking already fires `appointment.*`, so one booking is not handled twice |
| WIRE-APPT-013 | Privacy: `contact_meetings.title`, `.location` and `.join_url` are personal data (registry `contacts.notes_files`)             | the booking's title (the invitee's name), place and join link                                                                                                                                                                     | erasure: the row cascades with the contact; the audit delta records only that a title, place or link changed                                                                          |

## Not built, recorded

- The hourly drift check reads Google events only; Outlook drift is BL-200.
- Per-booking-type Outlook calendar; Teams links for personal Microsoft accounts.
- A watch channel or delta query for Outlook and for iCal (the fixed 15-minute read replaces them).
- Creating a meeting from the contact with the Appointments add-on, and changing or cancelling a booking from its Meetings row (BL-282); reminders per booking type (BL-283); persona wording of the nouns (BL-284).
- Real-account verification of Google and Microsoft (BL-199): everything here is exercised against local stand-ins written from the vendors' documentation.
