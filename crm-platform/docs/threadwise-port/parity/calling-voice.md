# Parity: calling-voice (CALL)

What differs from Reference 1.7.0, and why. Status per feature is in the [tracker](../tracker/vedant.md); the map is in
[wiring/calling-voice.md](../wiring/calling-voice.md). Built from the inventory and the reference source
(`WaCallingController`, `WaCallingService`, `WaCallingWebhookController`, `resources/js/calling/*`).

## Call logs (022, 036) and call analytics

Built from `CallLogsController`, `CallRecordingController::audio`, the `user/call-logs` index and detail views, `WaCallToAiLogBridge` and the
Call Logs card of the More page. Reference's page lists `ai_call_logs`, a mirror of `wa_calls` that its WhatsApp bridge fills on `connect` and
`terminate`; this port reads `wa_calls` and its ledger directly (the source of truth), so there is no mirror to fall behind.

| Id  | Reference                                                                                                                          | Here                                                                                                                                                                              | Why                                                                                           |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 022 | list of `ai_call_logs` (`workspace_id`), 25 to a page, newest first                                                                | list of `wa_calls`; same order, same page size, same status words (`completed`, `in-progress`, `no-answer`, `failed`; `declined` was written by the bridge but never offered)     | no mirror to keep in step; the AI mirror belongs with the AI call assistant                   |
| 022 | filters: range 24h / 7d / 30d (30d when unsaid, no upper bound), status, assistant, search in the two phone numbers                | the same range, status and search (the search also matches the contact's name); the assistant filter is replaced by number and teammate; direction and a custom range added       | no assistants exist yet; the others are the filters the task asked for (product extension)    |

Left off the call log, and recorded here so it is not lost: the assistant name and its link to the wizard, the AI cost column, the "Assistants"
button, the transcript and tool-call panels (all AI), and the More page's Call Logs card (`{n} calls / {m} min`): the More page is the shell's, and
the same numbers are the first two cards of the log.

### Not verified

- A real call: no WhatsApp number, microphone or Meta account was used. The log is exercised against rows written the way the webhook leaves
  them, plus a recording uploaded through the real upload route; the sample data comes from `seed:demo-calls`.
- The running Reference was not opened side by side (no Playwright twin run): the comparison is from its source.
- The audio element was exercised in jsdom with a faked media element (it asks once, renews an expired link once, shows a failure and a retry),
  not in a real browser with real playback, seeking or a screen reader.
- The query plan was read on 9 000 generated calls of two workspaces on a local Postgres, not on production data.
- Rows still `ringing` for hours (Meta never sent `terminate`) show as "Live now"; Reference had the same.

## Number readiness and onboarding (EXT-CALL-007 to 011)

Not in Reference: this is a product extension, requested so a freshly connected number can go from "connected" to "can place and receive
calls" without a developer touching Meta. Everything Reference did on the toggle is unchanged (same card, same button labels, same coexistence
refusal before Meta is asked, same local flags after Meta accepts); what is added or changed:

| Id           | Reference                                                                            | Here                                                                                                                                                                                    | Why                                                                                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 004 (words)  | Meta's refusal shown as the server's English sentence in a browser alert             | `CALL_META_REFUSED` carries a stable `reason`; the card shows it in the person's language (the long prerequisite text is kept, translated); an unnamed refusal still shows Meta's words | a sentence in English is no help to a workspace in another language, and parsing text is brittle |
| 004 (switch) | the local flag only changed by the toggle; it could drift from Meta forever          | a check reads Meta's calling status and brings the local flag in line (audited)                                                                                                         | the Call button must never show on a number Meta refuses, nor hide on one switched on there      |
| 008 (words)  | a dial refused for permission was recognised by the word "permission" in Meta's text | also by Meta's code 138006                                                                                                                                                              | text matching alone breaks when Meta rewords                                                     |
| ext          | nothing                                                                              | the readiness checklist, the automatic switch after a connect (off by default), the scheduled re-check, the `calls` field repair on the platform app (worker only), the test call       | the point of this work                                                                           |

Not verified (simulated): every Meta answer in the tests is a fake that returns what Meta's documentation describes (the calling settings
read, the app subscription read and write, the phone node, the token inspection). The Meta error codes mapped (141000, 138000, 138001, 138006,
138009, 190, 10, 200 to 299) are the ones this code has seen or that Meta documents; none was captured from a live refusal in this work. The
Playwright spec (`apps/web/e2e/calling-voice/readiness.spec.ts`) skips without a local account and was not run. The first real number is the check
that remains: [the manual checklist](../plan/calling-number-readiness-build.md#manual-test-with-a-real-number).

Decisions for the owner: whether `autoEnableOnConnect` should stay off by default (it is: nothing is switched on at Meta without an admin saying so);
whether a platform operator should be able to turn it on for every workspace; whether the scheduled check should stop for numbers nobody has
used for calling in 30 days; whether TURN should move to short-lived credentials (today's are a shared static secret served to everyone who may call).

## Not done, and what it costs

- **TURN is configuration, not a window global.** `CALLING_ICE_SERVERS` (a JSON list in the environment) is served to people who may call by `GET /v2/calling/ice-servers`; the provider uses it and falls back to the four public STUN servers when it is empty.

- **The AI answers nothing.** An inbound call rings the operators; there is no assistant to take it, no voicemail after the pickup delay and no
  bridge to close on terminate. A call nobody accepts ends as `missed` when the customer hangs up or Meta times it out.
- **Plan columns.** Minute caps and `access_call_recording` were never enforced in Reference and are not here; the one gate is the plan feature
  `waba_calling` (default on for existing plans).
- **Push with a poll fallback.** Each call step that changes who sees a ringing call writes a `call.changed` outbox event; a worker pushes it to the workspace room (a signal with the call's uuid) and the toast refetches. The poll stays as the fallback (4 seconds without the socket, 30 with it).
- **Browser parts are untested against a real microphone and Meta.** The peer is unit-tested with fakes; the API half is tested end to end
  against a fake Meta that records every request. A first call through a real number is the check that remains.
