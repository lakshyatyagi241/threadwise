Accepted decisions

70 unlimited routes, each with severity, rationale, owner and an expiry of 2027-01-31. Provider webhooks and OAuth callbacks are deliberately not throttled so real provider traffic is never dropped; they are rejected cheaply without a valid signature, secret or state. Critical and High can never be accepted.

Gate rules

R1 a new public route needs a census record. R2 an unlimited route needs a decision. R3 Critical and High are never accepted. R4 stale decisions are listed. R5 5xx under flood is High. R6 a failed probe blocks at its severity. R7 a deploy file that disables the limiter or trusts every proxy is Critical.
