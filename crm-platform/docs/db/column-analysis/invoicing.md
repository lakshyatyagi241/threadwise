## `credit_note_items`

**Purpose:** One line of a credit note: a negative-direction adjustment against an invoice line (or free text), with its own frozen price, tax and totals.

**Linked to:** crm/invoicing credit-notes.{repository,service}; web invoicing credit-notes builder/detail/PDF (Threadwise extension: Reference menu names Credit Notes but has no code).

**Facts:** RLS on+forced · plain · created in `20261008100200_inv_credit_notes.sql` · Drizzle `packages/db/src/schema/invoicing/tables-extra.ts`

**Design notes:** Per-line amounts duplicate the invoice_items shape (currency repeated on every line and on the header). tax_type/tax_name/tax_rate_percent/tax_components overlap: components is the authoritative split. line_total check only holds if all five money columns are consistent, good. No tax_rate_id FK (rate frozen as snapshot, intentional).

**Purpose:** Sales document sent to a buyer (tax invoice, proforma or receipt): draft then issued (numbered, frozen, public link) or void, with money totals, buyer/seller snapshots, payment ledger cache and delivery/view tracking.
