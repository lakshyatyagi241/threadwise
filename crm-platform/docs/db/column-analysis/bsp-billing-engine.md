## `bsp_credit_allocations`

**Purpose:** Per-WABA record that a customer WhatsApp Business Account was attached to the reseller credit line (Meta credit sharing), with status and failure details.

**Linked to:** bsp-billing-engine: reseller-credit.service, reseller.repository, reseller-platform.reader (platform admin), auto-attach-sweep.service, billing-mode.service. Reference TW-BSP-006/007.

**Facts:** RLS on+forced · plain · created in `20261001100800_bsp_threadwise_schema.sql` · Drizzle `packages/db/src/schema/bsp-billing-engine/tables.ts`

**Design notes:** Good: CHECK constraints on status/credit_source/currency. waba_id is a plain text pulled from wa_provider_configs.meta_json->>waba_id (no FK to a WABA table). currency duplicates reseller settings. created_by_operator_id references platform_operators(id) with a non-composite FK (platform-level, intended).
