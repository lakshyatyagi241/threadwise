# ADR-0000: Template

- **Status:** template
- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Deciders:** —

## Context

What forces are at play? What problem are we solving, and what constraints
(scale, team size, budget, compliance, deadline) bound the answer? Facts and
numbers, not opinions.

## Decision

What we are doing. One paragraph, active voice, present tense.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What gets easier or safer.

### Negative

- What gets harder, slower, or more expensive. Every real decision has some.
  An ADR with no negative consequences has not been thought through.

### Neutral

- Things that change without being better or worse.

## Alternatives considered

### Option B

Why not. Be specific — "too complex" is not a reason, "needs a second
datastore and an on-call rotation we do not have" is.

## Revisit when

The condition that would make us reopen this. An ADR without a trigger becomes
dogma.
