What this is

Liveness asks "is the process alive" and must never look at dependencies. Readiness asks "can it serve traffic now" and checks the database and Redis. A restart fixes a dead process, not a dead database, so mixing them turns a database blip into a full outage. Alerts are the other half: a healthcheck only marks a container, an alert tells a person.
