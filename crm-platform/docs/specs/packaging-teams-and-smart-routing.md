# Packaging of Teams and Smart routing (assignment, handoff, waiting queue)

Status: decided by the owner, built in PR "Smart routing add-on and packaging audit". Related: [api/teams-packaging.md](../api/teams-packaging.md),
[api/smart-routing-packaging.md](../api/smart-routing-packaging.md) (operator how-to), [teams-unification.md](teams-unification.md),
[conversation-handoff-and-assignment.md](conversation-handoff-and-assignment.md).

## 1. The packaging decision

Keep the **entry plan minimal** and let people expand by buying add-ons.

| Level                                          | What it holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | How it is switched on                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Basic plan (no auto-assignment)**            | Inbox, manual assignment, the Waiting queue and Claim, the Human handoff step to a named person or team, Return to bot (merged, no flag), leave and delegate, the Available/Offline switch, presence, the claim chat-limit warning, claim sets the contact owner, the queue alert, team hours                                                                                                | nothing: these have no plan flag                                                                       |
| **Add-on "Teams and permissions"** (unchanged) | `team_hierarchy`, `custom_roles`, and `teams` +10. Custom roles stay in it: they are not split out                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | draft plain pack `teams_permissions`                                                                   |
| **Add-on "Smart routing"** (new)               | `inbox_routing`: the auto-assignment master switch, rules, Everyone else, Try it, team methods (round robin, least loaded) and the team default assignee, business hours for rules, escalation timers; later also the waiting-queue timers, the arrival-waiting state and the offline/leave return. `inbox_assignment_sticky`: sticky agent, owner modes, continuity window, reassign prompt | draft plain pack `smart_routing`, or the same two flags on a higher plan tier, or a workspace override |

Rules of the design:

- **No new plan flags.** Smart routing is the two existing flags. A higher plan tier includes it by carrying the same two flags.
- **No add-on dependency engine.** Smart routing does not technically need Teams: a rule may assign to people. Only a destination that is a _team_ needs the
  `teams` cap, and the page says so. Teams without Smart routing and Smart routing without Teams are both valid (tested).
- **Locked, never hidden, never silently ignored.** Every locked control says which plan or add-on unlocks it ("Included in the Smart routing add-on or a higher
  plan") and links to `/account/plans` (`AddonLockLink`); the API answers 422 `PLAN_FEATURE_LOCKED` (`feature`, `reason`, `upgradeUrl`).
- **Nothing changes silently for an existing workspace.** See 3 and the decision on defaults below.

### Plan defaults (owner decision 2026-10-22)

There are **no paying clients on this system yet**; existing clients will later be migrated onto plans that carry the flags and add-ons they have today
(section 7). So there is no existing plan to protect, and the catalogue defaults of `inbox_routing` and `inbox_assignment_sticky` are now **`false`**: a plan
does not include automatic assignment unless it says so. (An earlier revision of this change kept them `true` to protect plan rows that lack the key; that
rule is withdrawn.)

What `resolveFeature` does with a plan row that lacks the key: the order is platform-owned workspace (always on), the workspace's own override, "no catalogue row
for the plan" (nothing is locked), then `storedFeature(plan.features, key)`, which returns the stored boolean or, when the row does not store the key,
**the catalogue default**. So a plan row without the keys now reads `false`; a plan must store `true` to include Smart routing. The sample plans seeded by
`seed-auth.ts` (fresh installs, inserted only when the slug is missing) now say it explicitly: Starter `false`/`false`, Growth and Scale `true`/`true`. This is the only
seeded data that changed; no data migration rewrites any plan row.

What existed in the real-data copy (`pg_dump` of `crm_owner`, counts only):

| Plan slug | Catalogue row?                  | `inbox_routing`                      | `inbox_assignment_sticky` | `team_hierarchy` | `custom_roles` | Workspaces |
| --------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------- | ---------- |
| `growth`  | no (the `plans` table is empty) | open: no row means nothing is locked | open                      | open             | open           | 2          |

Both workspaces have no platform-owned flag, no overrides and no add-ons (`workspace_addons` is empty). They keep working because a workspace whose plan slug has
no catalogue row is never locked. **The moment a `growth` row is created in the catalogue, they follow it**: create the plan with the flags the owner wants, or give
those workspaces an override. The operator and the owner's own test workspace should not depend on a plan at all: mark them **platform-owned** (always unlocked), or
set a workspace override of `inbox_routing` and `inbox_assignment_sticky` to `true` (Operator console, the workspace, Plans and overrides).

The operator's plan form already starts a new plan with both off (`NEW_PLAN_FEATURES_OFF`, the same as the default now). Exact steps to set the tiers are in
[api/smart-routing-packaging.md](../api/smart-routing-packaging.md) section 2.

## 2. Matrix

Legend. **Flag**: the plan feature that decides, or "base" for none. **RBAC**: the permission key and the system roles that hold it by default (Owner holds
every key; Admin every key except owner-only ones). **Scope**: the record scope the permission is checked at (`all` unless stated; settings keys have no scope).
**Operator**: where an operator configures what the workspace gets. **Settings**: where the workspace admin finds it. **OFF**: what happens without the flag.
**Downgrade**: what happens to stored data and in-flight work when the flag goes away.

Roles: Owner/Admin = everything; Manager = Agent plus `inbox.conversation.assign|bulk|priority`; Agent = `inbox.conversation.read|reply|note|assign_self|resolve|snooze|tag`,
`inbox.settings.read`; Viewer = `inbox.conversation.read` at team scope (and `read_unassigned`). `inbox.routing.manage`, `inbox.sla.manage`, `inbox.team.manage`
are Owner and Admin only by default; a custom role gets whatever it is given.

## 3. Impact analysis

**Existing workspaces.** There are no paying clients yet (section 1). No plan row is rewritten and the migration only inserts a draft pack. The two defaults are now
`false`, so a catalogue plan row that does not store the keys no longer includes Smart routing; workspaces whose plan has no catalogue row stay open. The visible changes are (a) labels and lock hints name "Smart routing", (b) a team method
change to round robin/least loaded is refused where the feature is off (before: stored and ignored), (c) the Auto-assignment tile no longer shows an "Upgrade" pill
for a plan without routing (the page has base controls), (d) a due escalation of a plan without routing is dropped.

| Event                                                         | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Plan downgrade mid-flight** (flags switched off)            | Stored rules, switches, owner mode, window, team methods are kept but not in effect (`getSettings` reports the in-effect value). Chats already Waiting or assigned stay as they are. Escalation timers stop (schedule cleared). Owner sync stops. New chats are not auto-assigned; they wait for a claim. Nothing is deleted, so switching the flags back restores everything. |
| **Add-on bought mid-month**                                   | `workspace_addons` row (`status=active`, `ends_at` null or in the future). `PlanEntitlementService.forOrg` reads the pack on every request (no cache in the API), so the API gates open at once; the web reads entitlements when the layout renders, so locks clear on the next page load. Chats already Waiting stay Waiting until a rule, claim or handoff moves them.       |
| **Add-on expired / cancelled**                                | `ends_at` in the past, or `status` not `active`: the pack is no longer merged; same as a downgrade. Tested: bought, ended and cancelled.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Operator override**                                         | A workspace override (`org_plan_state.overrides.features`) wins over plan and add-on in both directions (off beats a live add-on, on beats no add-on).                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Lapsed paid plan**                                          | Falls back to the free plan's flags (`paid_lapsed`). A pack the workspace bought is its own purchase and still applies on top. Tested.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Trial**                                                     | The plan's flags apply for the trial's length. `free_trial_expired` does not change entitlements by itself (the closed-account gate handles it).                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Teams cap lowered below the existing teams**                | Existing teams kept and editable, new ones refused; the Teams pack adds room. Tested.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Teams without Smart routing, and the reverse**              | Independent. Teams only: parent teams work, round robin is refused. Smart routing only: round robin works, a new parent link is refused. Tested.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Edition persona (`chat_team`, `sales_team`)**               | A preset hides tiles (the Auto-assignment tile is hidden for `sales_team`) but never decides entitlement: hidden means no lock is drawn, the API still honours the flags.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Platform-owned workspace, or a plan with no catalogue row** | Never locked (`resolveFeature`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Cached values**                                             | API: none for entitlements. Web: the layout's entitlement set per page load. Persona: about 30 s. Plan grace days: 30 s.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## 5. Operator checklist and Settings placement

**Operator console**

1. **Plans, the plan, Features tab, Chat line**: the two Smart routing switches (labelled "Smart routing: auto-assignment and rules" and "Smart routing: contact
   owner and sticky agent"). New plans start with them off. **Limits tab**: `routing_rules` (rules cap), `teams`.
2. **Add-ons**: the draft **Smart routing** pack (and **Teams and permissions**). Set the price (untick Free), tick Active, save. It lists the two features and no limits.
3. **A workspace**: the add-on panel gives or removes a pack; the plan/overrides panel sets per-workspace feature overrides (an override wins).
4. **Usage meters**: `teams` (count of teams), `routing_rules`.
5. Note: plain packs (Teams, Smart routing) are not part of a plan's `visibleAddons`: that table is for Marketplace apps (a trigger enforces it). A workspace on a paid
   plan sees plain add-ons in the add-on catalogue on the Plans page.

**Workspace Settings placement (audited)**

- Auto-assignment page: switches, rules, Everyone else, Try it, business hours, (later) the Waiting-queue section. In place.
- Teams / Team: method, default assignee, hours. In place; method and default assignee now lock with the add-on hint.
- Account menu: Available/Offline and **Time off** (step 1). In place.

## 6. Gaps found and fixed (one commit each)

1. Escalation sweep re-picked dropped-plan rows for ever and could starve other workspaces: schedule is dropped.
2. Team method stored and ignored without the feature: 422 plus a locked select; default assignee locked with the same hint.
3. Re-saving a team with its existing parent and manager was refused after the Teams add-on ended: only a new link is checked.
4. Locked controls did not name the add-on or link to where it is bought: shared `AddonLockLink`, paywall add-on line, copy in 21 locales.
5. "Send to queue" claimed the rules were available for a role that cannot read the inbox settings: the plan is asked as well.
6. Workspace business hours could not be edited with SLA but without routing: either feature opens them.
7. New plans started with every feature on, including the two Smart routing flags: they start off (existing plans untouched).

8. The registry default of the two flags is false (owner decision 2026-10-22); the seeded sample plans say it explicitly.

Found and left as is: plain add-ons do not appear in a plan's `visibleAddons` (Marketplace-only by design); the stale business-hours test above.

## 7. Client migration (for the later migration tool; not built)

A migrated client arrives with a feature set they have today. Map it to **a plan + add-ons + overrides**, in this order of preference, so the result is data an operator
can read and change:

1. **Pick the plan tier** whose stored flags match most of what they have (Basic: neither Smart routing flag; a higher tier: both on, the caps they paid for).
2. **Add-ons for the difference that is a product**: Smart routing missing from the tier but in use: a `workspace_addons` row for `smart_routing` (`status=active`,
   `starts_at` = migration day, `ends_at` = their current paid-through date or null). Teams (hierarchy, custom roles, more teams): the same with `teams_permissions`.
