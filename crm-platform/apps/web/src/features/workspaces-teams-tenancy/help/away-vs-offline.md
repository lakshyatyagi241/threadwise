## What changed

A person's availability used to read **Offline**. It now reads **Away**. The setting is the same: Away takes a person out of automatic assignment and leaves the chats they already hold with them. Only the word changed, in the account menu, the status pill, the pickers, the Team members list, the Try it results and these guides. Nothing stored was rewritten and existing choices keep working.

## Away is about chats, not connections

- **Away** means the person does not want new chats for now. They chose it, they are on leave, or they are outside their own working hours.
- A **WhatsApp number or device that is offline** is a connection problem, shown in the channel and device screens. It has nothing to do with a person's status.
- A customer who is not online has no effect on anyone's status either.

## Where you see it

- The status pill in the top bar and the Inbox: Available, Away, On leave until a date, or Away outside working hours.
- The assign menu, Send to queue and bulk assign: each person's state, and "Away, outside working hours" for someone whose schedule has them away.
- Team members and team lists: the dot beside each person.
- Automatic assignment settings: the people skipped are those who are away, busy, on leave or at their chat limit.
