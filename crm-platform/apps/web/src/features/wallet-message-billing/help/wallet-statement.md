## What it is for

The wallet screen shows your message balance and how it has moved in the last 30 days. It also explains how messages are billed for your workspace. The full statement lists every movement on the wallet: top-ups, message charges, refunds and manual adjustments, each with its balance after the change.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, go to **Business and billing** and select **Expenses, wallet and statement**.
2. Read the balance. When it is at or below your low-balance threshold, a warning appears at the top.
3. To add money, enter a whole amount in **Add money** or pick a quick amount. This opens the [Wallet top-up](/billing/checkout/wallet) checkout. If you open the top-up page without an amount, it asks for the amount first and shows the price and payment methods after you continue.
4. Select **Full statement** to open [the statement](/billing/wallet/statement).
5. On the statement, choose a type, a from and to date, or words to search for, then select **Apply**. **Reset** clears them.
6. Select **Export CSV** to download the rows that match the filters.

> The totals on the statement cover everything that matches the filters, not only the rows on the page. Amounts are shown exactly as the wallet holds them, up to three or four decimals (for example 0.958), so the rows, the totals and the balance add up. "Messages used this month" is the same number you see on the plan page and in Analytics.

## Options and settings

- **Type**: all movements, top-ups, earnings, message charges, refunds or adjustments.
- **From and to**: the date range. The end date cannot be before the start date.
- **Search**: matches the description or the reason of a movement.

## Plan and access

Seeing the balance needs the permission to view the wallet balance. The statement needs the permission to view the wallet statement, and the export needs the permission to export it. Owners and Admins hold all three by default. Without the balance permission the screen says you do not have access.

## If something looks wrong

- **The balance is not what you expect.** Recent activity can take a moment to arrive. Reload the page; the balance also updates on its own when a message is charged.
- **No rows match.** Clear the filters with **Reset**.
- **The export failed.** Try again with a narrower date range.
