## What it is for

Instagram accounts links your Instagram Business or Creator account to this workspace. Its direct messages then arrive in your team inbox, and you can reply, run flows and send reply-only broadcasts from here.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Instagram** under **Channels**. The screen lists your linked accounts.
2. Select **Add an Instagram account**. The screen checks whether an account you own is already connected to the Instagram connection service.
3. If one account is found, it is linked at once. If several are found, pick the one to link. If none is found, a sign-in window opens. Finish the sign-in there, and the account is linked when you return.
4. Each linked account shows its name, its handle, when it was last read and whether it is connected.

## Options and settings

- The external-link icon opens the account in the Instagram connection service, where it is managed.
- **Refresh profile** reloads the account's name, handle and picture.
- **Unlink** disconnects the account after you confirm. Its conversations and inbox data are removed from this workspace. The account itself stays connected in the service, so you can link it again later.

## Plan and access

You need the permission to view Instagram accounts to see this screen. Linking, refreshing and unlinking need the manage permission. Add and link actions appear only when the Instagram connection service is connected, which an administrator sets up in Admin, under Add-ons.

## If something looks wrong

- **"Instagram is not connected. An administrator has to connect it."** The connection service is not set up. Ask your administrator.
- **"Could not link that account."** Check that the account is a Business or Creator account, then try again.
- **The account shows Needs re-auth.** Its access has expired. Open it in the connection service, sign in again and refresh it here.
- **Unlinking removed conversations.** This is expected. Link the account again to bring new messages in.
- For sending to several conversations at once, see [Instagram broadcast](help:instagram-broadcast).
