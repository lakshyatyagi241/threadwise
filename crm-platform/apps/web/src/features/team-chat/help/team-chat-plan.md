## What it is for

**Team chat** is a feature of your plan. When your plan includes it, your workspace can use the internal chat described in [Team chat](help:team-chat-overview): channels, direct messages, files, mentions and invitations. This page explains what the plan decides.

## What the plan decides

- **Whether team chat is available at all.** The feature covers all of team chat, reading as well as writing. If your plan does not include it, the chat's requests are refused and the guide for it is hidden.
- **How many channels the workspace can hold.** The plan can cap the total number of channels, public and private together. A cap of 0 means no limit. Deleting a channel frees its place.
- Direct messages and the **#general** channel that every workspace starts with do not count against the cap.

## How to use it

1. To check what your plan includes, open **Plans** and look under **What your plan includes** for team chat. See [Plans](help:account-plans).
2. To create a channel, open **Team Chat** and use **Create channel** in the left column. If you have reached the cap, the channel is not created.
3. To make room, delete a channel you no longer need (you need channel management rights for that), or ask for a plan with a higher channel limit.

## If something looks wrong

- **Team chat does not load, or every action fails.** Your plan may not include the feature. Ask a workspace owner to check the plan.
- **A new channel is refused although the name is free.** The workspace may be at its channel limit. Delete an unused channel or upgrade.
