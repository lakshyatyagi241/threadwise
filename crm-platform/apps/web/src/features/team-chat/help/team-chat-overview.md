## What it is for

Team chat is the internal chat for your workspace. Use it to talk with colleagues about a customer or a task, away from customer conversations, which live in the [Team inbox](help:team-inbox-overview).

## How to use it

1. Open **Team Chat**. The left column lists channels and direct messages, the right lists the members, and the conversation is in the middle. The first channel opens by itself.
2. To write, type in the box at the bottom and press Enter. Use Shift+Enter for a new line, and `@` to mention a teammate.
3. To reply to one message, choose **Reply** on it. To react, choose **React** and pick an emoji.
4. To attach a file, use the attach button or drop it on the conversation. Images, PDFs, Word, Excel, text and CSV files up to 20 MB are accepted.
5. To message someone privately, select them in the members list and choose **Message**. A direct message is created if it does not exist yet.
6. To search a channel, open **Search messages** from the channel header. Type at least two characters.

## Options and settings

- A channel is **Channel** (public, seen by everyone in the workspace) or **Private** (invite only). Give it a lowercase name, and an optional description.
- **Channel members** shows who is in the channel. Channel admins can add or remove people, and anyone can leave.
- **Pinned messages** lists what the channel has pinned. Pin or unpin a message from its menu.
- You can edit your own message for 15 minutes after sending it. Delete a message from its menu; it is removed for everyone.
- **Invite a teammate** sends an invitation for an admin to approve. **Pending invitations** shows requests and lets admins approve or decline them.

## Plan and access

Team chat is open to every member of the workspace. Creating a channel and approving invitations need the matching channel management rights. Only a channel's own admins can manage its members. The **#general** channel cannot be deleted.

## If something looks wrong

- **Your message did not send.** A message that failed shows **Retry** and **Discard**. Check your connection.
- **You cannot delete a channel.** Only channels you may manage can be deleted, and #general is protected.
- **An edit is refused.** Messages can only be edited for 15 minutes, and only by their author.
- **A channel name is taken.** Choose another name. Names use lowercase letters, digits, dashes and underscores.
