## What it is for

AI Studio is the starting page for everything that talks to your customers for you. It does not build anything itself: each card takes you to the screen that does.

## How to use it

1. Open **AI Studio** in the Automation group of the menu.
2. Pick what you want to build:
   - **AI agent** answers from the knowledge you give it, such as documents and web pages. Select **Create an AI agent**, or **Your AI agents** to open the ones you have.
   - **Chatbot** is a guided conversation with buttons, questions, cards and files. Select **Website chat bot** to build one for your website, or **WhatsApp bot** to build one for WhatsApp. Both open the Simple builder.
   - **Workflow** is the advanced canvas for tags, conditions, webhooks and CRM steps. Select **Create a workflow**, or **Your flows** to see what you already have.
3. A chatbot has three tabs: **Build**, **Customize** and **Deploy**. Publish it when it is ready, then copy the embed code or the link from **Deploy**.

## Plan and access

You need the **Automation** plan feature and the right to view flows to see the page. Each card also needs the right to create what it starts: an AI agent needs the right to create AI training, a chatbot or workflow needs the right to create flows. A card you cannot use says so.

## If something looks wrong

- **A card says you cannot use it.** Ask a workspace admin for the matching right.
- **AI agent opens a page that asks for a plan.** AI agents need the AI assistants plan feature.
