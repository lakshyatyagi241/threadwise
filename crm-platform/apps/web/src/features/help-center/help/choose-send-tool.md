## What it is for

Three screens in the Campaigns area can message many contacts at once, and they overlap. This guide helps you choose one for your first send and lists what to check before you press the button.

## Which one to use

- **Campaigns** is the most flexible. The message can be plain text, a rich message with a header, footer, media and buttons, an approved template, or a flow. The audience can be tags, groups, contacts, typed numbers or an uploaded CSV. It can go out now, once later or on repeat, supports an A/B test, and has per-campaign pacing. See [Campaigns](help:campaigns-overview).
- **Campaigns with a Meta number** (the guided four-step flow) sends one approved template to contacts, groups, segments or a CSV of numbers, now, at a time and time zone you choose, or on repeat. See [Create a campaign](help:campaigns-create).
- **Scheduled messages** queues a message for later or on a repeat, as plain text, a template, media or a location. It has a **Send a test first** option and lets you pause, resume or run a schedule by hand. It needs the Scheduled messages feature on your plan. See [Scheduled messages](help:scheduled-messages-overview).

A simple rule: if you want to send an approved template to a list once, use Broadcasts. If you want to write a different kind of message, compare two versions, or upload a list of numbers, use Campaigns. If the point is a repeating, pausable schedule, use Scheduled messages.

## Before your first send

1. **Connect a WhatsApp number.** See [WhatsApp numbers](help:whatsapp-numbers-overview).
2. **Have a template ready if you need one.** Only approved templates can be picked. See [Message templates](help:message-templates-overview).
3. **Know your audience.** Tags and groups are on the Contacts screens. See [Contacts](help:contacts-overview) and [Contact groups](help:contact-groups). To message everyone who matches a rule, see [Message the people in a segment](help:segment-to-audience).
4. **Expect opted-out contacts to be skipped.** They are left out of the send, so the audience count can be lower than the people you picked.
5. **Check your wallet.** A send can be refused when the wallet cannot cover it. The balance is shown in the bar at the top of the page, and [Wallet top-up](help:wallet-top-up) explains how to add money.
6. **Preview it.** Campaigns show a live preview while you build. Scheduled messages can send one test message to a number you enter.

## After you launch

- Open the send to follow sent, delivered, read and failed counts. See [Why a campaign shows few reads](help:campaign-read-rate) for how to read them.
- To stop or restart a campaign, see [Stop, resume or resend a campaign](help:campaign-stop-resume).
- To compare two versions of a campaign, see [A/B test a campaign](help:campaign-ab-test).
- To repeat a send, see [Recurring sends and pausing them](help:recurring-sends-pause).

## If something looks wrong

- **The form will not submit.** A message beside the button or under the preview says what is missing: a name, a message, an audience or a date.
- **A template is not in the list.** Only approved templates are offered. Check its status under **Templates**.
- **You cannot see a screen.** Each screen needs its own right and may depend on your plan. Ask a workspace admin.
