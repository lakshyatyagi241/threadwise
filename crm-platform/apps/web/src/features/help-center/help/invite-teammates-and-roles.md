## What it is for

This guide takes you from "I need someone to help me" to a working teammate: choose a role, send the invite, check they got in, and put them in a team. The screens themselves are described in [Team members](help:team-members), [Roles and permissions](help:roles) and [Teams](help:teams).

## Choose a role first

Every person in the workspace has a role, and the role decides what they can see and do. The five preset roles are:

- **Owner**: full access, including billing.
- **Admin**: full workspace access except billing.
- **Manager**: sees all teams' conversations, can assign and resolve them, and works with contacts, templates, labels and the dashboards.
- **Agent**: handles conversations and the day-to-day records such as contacts, deals and tasks, but not workspace settings.
- **Viewer**: read-only. A Viewer can read the conversations of their own teams and nothing else.

Most support staff only need **Agent**. You can always change a role later. To see exactly what a role allows, or to make a custom role, open **Settings**, then **Roles and permissions**.

> You can only give a role that is at or below your own. Only an Owner can hand out the Owner and Admin roles.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings**, go to **Team and permissions** and select **Team members**, or go to `/team-members`.
2. Select **Invite teammate**.
3. Enter their **Full name** and **Email**, choose a **Workspace role** and select **Send invite**.
4. They receive an email with an invite link. The link works once and expires after a few days. On the page it opens, they choose a password (at least 8 characters) and select **Accept invitation**.
5. If your server cannot send email, the screen shows a one-time link instead. Copy it straight away, because it is shown only once, and send it to them yourself.

There is also a second **Team members** screen at `/settings/team`. There you select **Add member** and set the person's password yourself instead of sending a link. Use it when the person cannot receive email, and hand over the password in person.

## Check that it worked

1. Stay on **Team members**. A person who has not accepted yet is marked **pending**. Select **Resend invite** if the email did not arrive.
2. Once they have signed in, their row shows whether they are online, away, busy or offline.
3. To change a role later, pick another one in the role list on their row. To stop someone's access, select **Remove from workspace**.

## Put them in a team

Teams decide who shares which conversations. Open **Settings**, then **Teams**, select **Create team**, name it, choose how new conversations are handed out and tick its members. Existing teams can be edited with the pencil on their card.

## Plan and access

Everyone in the workspace can read the member list. Inviting, resetting passwords and removing members need the permission to invite members. Changing roles needs the permission to assign roles. Owners and Admins have both by default. Your plan limits the number of seats, shown next to the invite button as "used of limit seats". Pending invites count as seats, removed members do not.

## If something looks wrong

- **The invite says all seats are in use.** Your plan's seat limit is reached. Remove someone who no longer needs access, or upgrade from [Plan and usage](help:plan-and-usage).
- **The role you want is not in the list.** You can only hand out roles at or below your own, and a custom role only if you hold every permission in it.
- **The invited person says the link no longer works.** Invite links work once and expire. Select **Resend invite** to send a fresh one.
- **You cannot change your own role.** Ask another Owner or Admin. The workspace always keeps at least one Owner.
