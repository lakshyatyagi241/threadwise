## Where to find it

Select the question mark icon just above Settings in the main menu. On a phone, open the menu first. The help center lists the guides for the features available to you, grouped by the same areas as the menu.

## Searching

Type in the search box at the top of the help center. A guide matches when every word you typed appears in its title, its keywords, its section headings or its summary. Title matches come first. Clear the box to see all guides again.

## The question mark on a page

On a screen that has a guide, a small question mark button appears in the bar at the top of the page, next to the settings gear when the screen has one.

1. Select the button to read a short summary of the screen.
2. Select **Read the guide** to open the full guide.
3. Press Escape, or select outside the summary, to close it.

## Jumping to any page with Ctrl K

Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) on any page, or select the search button in the top bar, to open the page search. Type part of a menu entry or a Settings tile, such as "templates" or "tax", use the up and down arrow keys and press Enter to open it. It only lists pages your role and plan allow you to open. If nothing matches and the screen you are on has its own search box, press Enter to search there with what you typed. On a screen that uses Ctrl K for its own search box, such as Contacts or the help center, the shortcut goes to that box instead.

## Why some guides are not listed

The help center only shows guides for features you can use:

- A feature that is not part of your plan has no guide here. Upgrading your plan adds its guide the next time a page loads.
- A screen you are not allowed to open has no guide here either.
- If a feature is turned off for the whole platform, its guide goes with it.

If a guide is not in your language yet, it is shown in English with a note at the top.
