## What your workspace is for

Your workspace keeps your customers, conversations, sales and automations in one place. Everything you see here belongs to your workspace only, and what you see in the menu depends on your plan and your role.

## Finding your way around

- **The menu on the left** has one icon for each area: Platform, Inbox & Chat, CRM, Sales, Campaigns, Automation, Store and Marketplace. Point at an icon, or select it, to open a panel with every screen in that area.
- **Settings** is the gear at the bottom of the menu. Everything you configure lives there, grouped into sections such as channels, data, business and people.
- **Help** is the question mark just above Settings. It opens this help center.
- **The account menu** at the top right holds your account, language, theme and Log out.
- **The bar at the top of a page** shows the workspace balance and the bell for notifications. On pages that have a guide, a small question mark button appears there too.

## Why a screen or a guide can be missing

Two things decide what you see:

- **Your plan.** A screen your plan does not include shows an **Upgrade** mark in the menu, and its guide is not listed in the help center. Open [Plans and pricing](/account/plans) to compare plans.
- **Your role.** Screens you are not allowed to use are hidden from the menu. If you need one, ask a workspace admin to change your role.

> A guide appears in the help center as soon as the feature it describes is available to you, and disappears when the feature is not part of your plan.
