## What it is for

Reports summarise how the support desk is doing over a window you choose.

## How to use it

1. Open **Support**, then **Reports**.
2. Pick **7d**, **30d**, **90d** or **1y** at the top.
3. Read the tiles (tickets, resolved, average first reply, average resolution, SLA compliance), the daily volume chart, the **By status** split and the **Top agents** table.
4. Select **Download CSV** to export the raw ticket rows for the window.
