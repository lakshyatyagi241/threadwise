## What it is for

Use this page to ask the platform support team for help. Every message becomes a ticket you can follow and reply to.

## How to use it

1. Open **Support** from the menu, or go to `/support`.
2. Under **What's it about**, pick a reason: **Something is broken**, **Message delivery**, **Template approval**, **Billing & plans**, **Integration help**, **Account access** or **Something else**.
3. Fill in **Your name**, **Reply email**, **Subject** and **Tell us what happened**. Be specific: the steps, what you expected and what happened.
4. Select **Send to support**. A message confirms the ticket number.
5. Your recent tickets are listed under **Recent tickets**, with how many are open. Select one to open its thread.

## Following a ticket

- The thread shows your messages and the **Support team** replies in order. A status pill shows whether the ticket is open, in progress, pending, resolved or closed.
- Write in the **Reply** box and select **Send reply** to add details. Replying to a resolved ticket reopens it.
- A closed ticket takes no more replies: select **Open a new ticket**.

> Attachments are not sent. Only the ticket text reaches support, so paste links or error text into the message.

## If something looks wrong

- **The form says some values are not valid.** Check the email address and that the subject and message are filled in.
- **You cannot reply.** The ticket is closed. Open a new one.
