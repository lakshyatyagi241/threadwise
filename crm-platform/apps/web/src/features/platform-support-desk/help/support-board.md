## What it is for

The team kanban shows tickets as cards in four columns: **New**, **In progress**, **Awaiting customer** and **Resolved**.

## How to use it

1. Open **Support**, then **Team inbox**.
2. Drag a card to another column to change its status. A toast says **Moved.** Dropping on **Resolved** stamps the resolved time.
3. For keyboard use, every card has a **Move to** menu with the same columns.
4. Choose **Mine only** to see just the tickets assigned to you, or **Show all tickets** to see all of them.

## If something looks wrong

- **A move is refused.** Your role may be read-only (auditor), or the ticket changed meanwhile and was reloaded: try again.
