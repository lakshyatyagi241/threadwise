## What it is for

The support inbox lists every ticket customers raised from their workspaces. Operators with the support or super admin role can change tickets; auditors can only read them.

## How to use it

1. Open **Support**, then **Inbox**. The tiles show **Open**, **Unassigned**, **Resolved 24h** and **All-time**.
2. Narrow the list with **Status**, **Priority** and **Agent** (including **Assigned to me** and **Unassigned**), or search by ticket number or customer.
3. Select a row to open the ticket panel. It shows the customer, workspace, SLA targets and the thread.
4. Write in the reply box and select **Send reply** to answer the customer by email and in their thread, or tick **Internal note** and select **Add note** for a note the customer never sees.
5. Change **Status** and **Priority** with the pills, or choose an agent under **Assign to** and select **Save**.
6. Under **Run playbook**, pick a playbook and select **Run** to apply its steps. See [Reply playbooks](help:support-operator-playbooks).

## Good to know

- If the reply was saved but the email could not be sent, the message is marked **email not sent**.
- If someone else changed the ticket first, it reloads and you apply your change again.
- Moving a ticket to resolved stamps the resolved time; moving it back to open clears it.
- **Kanban view** opens the [Team kanban](help:support-operator-board).
