## What it is for

SLA policies say how fast a ticket needs its first reply and its resolution. The board also lists tickets that already missed a target.

## How to use it

1. Open **Support**, then **SLA board**. The tiles show **Policies**, **Open breaches**, **At risk now** and **Compliance** over 7 days.
2. To add a policy, fill **Name**, **1st reply (min)** and **Resolve (min)**, optionally tick **Business hours only** or **Set as default**, then select **Add policy**.
3. Select **Edit** on a policy to change it, or **Delete** to remove it.
4. **Recent breaches** lists the ticket, the type of breach, how far over the target it went and the severity.

## Good to know

- Breaches are detected automatically about every five minutes, once per ticket and type.
- Business-hours policies use the customer workspace's own opening hours.
- Every policy change is written to the audit log.
