## What it is for

This page lists the workspaces that have raised tickets, busiest first, so you can see who needs attention.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Customers** page under **Support**. The tiles show **Customers**, **Total tickets**, **Open** and **Top open**.
2. Type in **Filter workspace** to find one, then select **Open**.
3. The workspace page shows total, open and resolved counts, average first reply and resolution, and the **Ticket history** with status, priority, assignee and time to resolve.
