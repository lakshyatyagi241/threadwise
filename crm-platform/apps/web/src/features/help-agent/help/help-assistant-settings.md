## What it is for

The Help assistant is off until the workspace owner or an admin turns it on. This page is the switch, with a plain statement of what is shared when people use it.

## How to turn it on

1. Open **Settings** and select **Help assistant**.
2. Read **What is shared when someone uses it**.
3. Turn on **Turn on the Help assistant for this workspace**. Turning it on confirms that your workspace agrees to share this with the AI provider.
4. Turn it off the same way. The change applies at once, even to a task that is running.

## Who can use it

Only roles with the permission **Use the Help assistant** see the Ask button. Owners and admins have it. To give it to another role, open **Settings**, **Roles** and tick the permission. The switch itself needs the permission **Switch the Help assistant on or off for the workspace**, which only owners and admins hold.

## What it costs

Each step the assistant takes uses AI tokens. They count against your workspace's monthly AI allowance when the platform's key is used, or against your own key when your plan lets you add one. The AI usage screen shows the total.

## Plan requirement

The assistant is a plan feature. If your plan does not include it, this tile is locked and the Ask button does not appear.

## If something looks wrong

- The switch is on but a person sees no Ask button: check that their role has the permission **Use the Help assistant**.
- People see "The assistant is not set up yet": the platform owner has not connected an AI provider. Ask them.

## Get it as an app

The Help assistant is also sold as an app in the **Marketplace**. A workspace whose plan already includes it keeps it as it is, with nothing to buy or install. A workspace without it can subscribe to the app and install it to switch it on. See [Marketplace](help:marketplace-overview).
