## What it is for

The assistant answers "how do I..." and can do the clicking for you. It works inside the app with your own sign-in, so it can only do what you can do, and it never leaves the app.

You see it as the **Ask** button at the bottom corner of most screens, and as the **Ask the assistant** card at the top of the Help center. It is there only when your plan includes it, a workspace owner or admin has turned it on, and your role may use it.

## Ask for something

1. Select **Ask**, or press Ctrl or Cmd with Shift and L.
2. Read the short notice the first time. It explains what is sent to the AI provider.
3. Type what you want, for example "add the tag VIP to a contact". The boxes under the field are examples for the screen you are on.
4. Guides that match your words appear under the box. Select **Show steps** to read the steps, or **Open guide** to read the whole guide.
5. Select **Do it for me** to let the assistant do it, or **Show me instead** to have it circle the control you need without pressing anything.

## While it works

- The list shows each step in your language. **Stop** ends the task at once.
- Before it saves, sends, deletes, pays, invites someone, changes roles, permissions, settings or billing, connects or disconnects a channel, or starts a broadcast, it stops and shows a card with **Allow once** and **Deny**. Deny is selected first, so pressing Enter never allows anything. If you deny, it does not try another way.
- It asks you a question when it needs something only you know. It never types passwords, card numbers or codes: it asks you to type them.

> The assistant is off on the Inbox, team chat and support threads. Those screens show text written by customers and other people, and the assistant must not read it.

## What is sent and what is hidden

The text of the page you are on and your request go to the AI provider the platform uses. Phone numbers, e-mail addresses, long numbers, secrets and the personal fields of forms are hidden before that. Names and other free text on a page are not hidden. Only the number of steps, the outcome and how many actions you allowed are kept, not your request.

## Limits

- A task has a limited number of steps and time, and each person has a daily number of tasks. When a limit is reached, the panel says so.
- It cannot use screens that need dragging, such as the flow builder canvas. It then tells you the steps instead.
- Large or vague requests work worse than one clear request.

## If something looks wrong

- The panel shows a message and a **Request ID** when the service fails. Give that ID to your admin or the platform owner.
- No **Ask** button? Your plan, your role or the workspace setting does not allow it. An owner or admin can switch it on under **Settings**, **Help assistant**. See [Turn on the Help assistant](help:help-assistant-settings).
