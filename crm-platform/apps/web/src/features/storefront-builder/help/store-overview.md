## What it is for

The Store area is where you sell from your own shop page and from WhatsApp. The overview shows your last 30 days: revenue, orders, average order value, products and storefront visits. Below it are the sales by day, your bestselling products and the newest orders.

## How to use it

1. Open **Store** in the menu. If you have no shop yet, you are sent to set one up.
2. Read the five figures at the top, then the **Sales trend** chart. Each day shows revenue and orders. Hover or focus a day to read its numbers.
3. Select **Add product** to open the product form.
4. To set up or change a shop, open **Connection** in the left column. It opens the shop list, where you add, edit, preview, share or delete a shop.
5. To change how the shop looks, open **Storefront** in the left column and select **Save** after your changes. **View live** opens the public shop.

> The left column lists the store sections. Items marked **Coming soon** are not built yet, and they cannot be opened.

## Options and settings

- The **Store** column shows the shop name, whether it is live, the public URL with **Open** and **Copy**, and the WhatsApp number it sends from.
- When you have more than one shop, **Switch shop** changes which shop the screens show.
- Reviews and Products open their own pages from the same column.
- Storefront settings cover the theme, brand, currency, shipping, the payment link and abandoned-cart recovery. The custom domain is checked with **Verify now**.

## Plan and access

You need the store dashboard permission to see this area. Storefront settings need the storefront read permission to view and the manage permission to change. Shops are created, edited and deleted with the shop management permission. A shop needs a connected WhatsApp number, so the set-up screen asks you to connect one first.

## If something looks wrong

- **"Connect WhatsApp first."** Connect a WhatsApp number from **Channels**, then return to the shop set-up.
- **The overview says there is no shop yet.** Set up your store from **Connection**.
- **"Address taken" when you save a shop.** Another shop already uses that address. Change the shop name or the storefront address.
- **A custom domain is not verified.** Add the CNAME record your DNS provider needs, then select **Verify now**.
- Shopify and WooCommerce stores have their own screens: see [Shopify store](help:shopify-store) and [WooCommerce store](help:woocommerce-store).
