## What a business type does

Every workspace has a **business type**. The default, **Generic**, shows every screen your plan and role allow, exactly as the product always has. A business type can also tidy the workspace by leaving out screens that kind of business rarely needs, so the menu, the Settings page, the tabs on a contact and the cards on Home stay short.

## What is left out

When your business type hides a module, it is not drawn in these places:

- the main menu and the search box (the command palette);
- the tiles on the Settings page;
- the tabs and the action buttons on a contact, and the matching cards beside it;
- the cards on Home;
- the guides in the help center that only explain hidden screens.

## What hidden does not mean

- **Nothing is deleted.** Your contacts, deals, appointments and every other record stay where they were.
- **Nothing is locked or closed.** A hidden screen still opens from a link or a bookmark when your role and your plan allow it. Hidden is a choice about the menu, not a security rule.
- **Nothing is unlocked.** A business type never adds a feature your plan or your role does not include. A screen that needs a plan feature or an add-on shows the same upgrade note it always did once it is shown.
- **No upgrade nudges for hidden screens.** A screen your business type hides shows no lock icon and no upgrade note.

## If a screen you expect is missing

1. Check your role first: a screen your role may not open is never in your menu, whatever the business type.
2. Check the plan and the Marketplace: some screens belong to an add-on that has to be turned on.
3. If both are fine, your business type may be hiding it. The page in Settings for changing the business type and revealing hidden modules is not part of this release yet.
