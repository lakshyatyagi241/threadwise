## What it is for

Sales Activities is the one place where you decide how the activities your team logs behave. There are seven built in: Task, Meeting, Phone, Email, Reminder, SMS and Chat. You can add activities of your own, such as a site visit. For the ones that have a setup screen you choose what people may do with them, the task types a task can have, and the outcomes a finished task, meeting or call can end with.

Nothing changes for your team until you edit something here. The starting values match how tasks, meetings and calls already worked.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, go to **Data** and select **Sales Activities**.
2. Find the activity in the list and select **Edit activity**. Email, Reminder, SMS and Chat have nothing to set up.
3. Change a switch, or add, remove or reorder an item in a list. Every change saves as you make it.
4. Select **Create activity** to add your own. Give it a name, pick an icon, and say whether it works like a task, a meeting or a phone call. It then starts with the switches of that kind.

## What you can set

**Task.** The switches are: show in the calendar, allow people to mark tasks completed, and allow people to edit the completed date and time of a task. Task types are the labels a task can carry, such as Follow up or Call reminder. The outcomes a finished task can have, such as Interested or Not able to reach, are listed under them. **Predict outcomes for this activity** switches the outcome pickers on tasks on or off. You can add, rename, drag to reorder and remove outcomes right in the drawer; this needs the permission to manage task outcomes, which owners and admins have. A removed outcome that tasks already carry is hidden rather than deleted, so those tasks keep showing it, and adding the same name again brings it back. An outcome no task uses is deleted. The old Task outcomes screen is gone; its address now opens this page.

**Meeting.** The same switches, plus allow check-in and check-out from the mobile app, and its own list of outcomes. A meeting gets an **Add outcome** button on the contact's Meetings tab once the Meeting activity allows completing.

**Phone.** Only the list of outcomes, which is what you pick when you log a call. The seven built-in outcomes are there to start with. When you add your own, say how it counts in call reports (for example "no answer"). The call reports keep using the built-in outcomes.

> Removing a task type or an outcome only takes it out of the pickers. Tasks, meetings and calls that already have it keep showing it.

Adding a name you removed earlier brings the old item back, so its history stays together. A default activity cannot be renamed or removed, but its switches and lists can change.

## Where the choices show up

- **Tasks.** The task form and the task panel offer the task types. Outcomes come from the Task outcomes list in the drawer. When completing is switched off, the tick box on a task is disabled, but a task that is already done can still be reopened.
- **Meetings.** The contact's Meetings tab shows **Add outcome**, then the outcome and notes, with **Edit** and **Reopen**.
- **Calls.** The Log a call form lists your Phone outcomes.

## Limits you should know

- **Mobile check-in and the activities calendar are not available yet.** The check-in switch and the calendar switch are saved, but the mobile app has no meeting check-in and there is no activities calendar yet. They start to matter when those exist.
- The call logs list still shows the built-in outcome of a call. The call itself keeps the outcome you picked.
- At most 20 custom activities, and 50 items in a list.

## Who can use it

People with the permission to see the settings can open the page. Changing anything needs the permission to manage sales activities, which owners and admins have. Everyone who works with tasks, contacts or calls sees the lists in the pickers without needing it.

## If something looks wrong

- An outcome is missing from a picker: it may have been removed, or outcomes may be switched off for that activity. Check **Predict outcomes for this activity**.
- You cannot tick a task: **Allow users to mark this activity as completed** may be off for Task.
- "Someone else changed this activity": reload the page and make your change again.
