## What it is for

The Account page is where you manage yourself: your photo, your name, the email you sign in with, your phone number, and how the app looks and reads for you. A list on the left links to the other account pages, such as your plan and your password and security settings.

## How to use it

1. Open the account menu at the top right and choose **Account**, or open **Settings**, then **Business and billing**, then **Account**. You land on **Profile**.
2. To change your photo, select **Change photo** under the picture on the left and pick a JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF image. It is shrunk in your browser before it is uploaded. **Remove photo** puts your initials back.
3. Under **Personal info**, edit your **Full name** and your **Display name**, which is how your name appears to teammates. If you never set one, it starts as your first name.
4. Edit your **Phone** if you want to: choose the country first, then type the number.
5. Choose your **Language** and your **Theme** (**Paper**, **Bright** or **Dark**). For language, **Use the default** leaves it to the app's default.
6. Select **Save changes**. Only the fields you changed are sent. **Reset** puts every field back to what was last saved.

## Changing your email

Your email is shown read-only, with a badge that says **Verified** or **Not verified**. Select **Change email**, type the new address and your current password, then select **Send confirmation link**. Your sign-in email changes only after you open the link sent to the new address. Until then the page shows which address is waiting.

## The workspace timezone

The **Timezone** box belongs to the workspace, not to you, and says so with a **(workspace)** tag. Everyone can see it, but only some roles can change it. If you cannot, the box is greyed out.

## Other account pages

The list on the left has two groups. **Account** holds Profile, Plans, Plan and usage and Branding. **Security** holds Password & security and **Delete account**. Deleting your account is permanent. It asks for a confirmation phrase and your password, and is refused if you are the only member or the only administrator.

## Plan and access

Every signed-in member can open their own account. For the other pages, see [Plans](help:account-plans), [Message footer](help:account-branding) and [Password and two-step sign-in](help:account-sign-in-security).

## If something looks wrong

- **The photo is refused.** Use a JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF image under 20 MB. The message on the page names the reason.
- **My new email did not take effect.** Open the confirmation link that was sent to the new address.
- **Language or theme did not change.** Save the form first, since they apply when you select **Save changes**.
