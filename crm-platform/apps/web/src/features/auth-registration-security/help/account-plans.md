## What it is for

The Plans page shows every plan on offer to this workspace, which one you are on today, and what each includes. A plan belongs to the workspace, so a change here applies to everyone in it. Every **Upgrade** link in the app leads here.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Business and billing**, then **Plan and usage**. You can also choose **Plan & usage** from the list on the Account page.
2. Read the banner at the top. It says whether your plan is active and for how long, whether a free trial is running or has ended, or whether your plan has expired and paid features are paused.
3. If a **Monthly** and **Yearly** switch is shown, choose the billing period. The page opens on Yearly and shows the saving.
4. Look at the plan cards. Each shows the name, price and period, a short feature list (use **Show all** for the rest), and one button. Your own plan is marked **Current plan**.
5. Select the button on the plan you want.
6. Scroll down for the add-ons on offer, the **Compare plans** table and the **Frequently asked questions**. **See usage and limits** at the bottom opens `/account/usage`.

### What the button does

- **Upgrade to** a dearer paid plan opens the checkout, where you pay.
- **Switch to** a cheaper or equal-priced plan, or a free plan, asks you to confirm and then moves the workspace straight away. Nothing is deleted, but anything above the new limits cannot be added to until you are back under them, and the page lists those items. When the new plan is a paid one, the paid time you have left stays on your workspace.
- **Talk to sales** is shown on plans that are arranged with the sales team.

## Stopping auto-renew

When a renewing subscription exists, the page says when it renews and offers **Cancel auto-renew**. Your plan stays active until it expires and then does not renew.

## Plan and access

Everyone in the workspace can see the plans. Only workspace owners and admins, who hold the permission to manage the plan, can use the buttons. For everyone else the buttons are off and a note explains why. Add-ons are covered in [Add-ons](help:workspace-addons).

## If something looks wrong

- **A plan I expected is missing.** The page lists only the active plans set up for your workspace. If the list is empty, a platform administrator has to create plans.
- **A switch is refused.** The confirmation shows a message, for example that the plan cannot be selected right now. Close it and try another plan.
- **The buttons are greyed out.** Your role cannot change the plan. Ask a workspace owner or admin.
