## What it is for

The Branding page controls the **message footer**: the line added at the bottom of plain text and interactive messages sent from your automations, scheduled sends and team inbox replies. Approved templates carry their own text and skip the footer.

On a plan without a custom footer, your messages end with the platform's default footer. On a plan that removes branding, you choose your own line, or no line at all.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Business and billing**, then **Branding**. You can also pick **Branding** from the list on the Account page.
2. Type your line in **Custom footer**. It can be up to 60 characters, and a counter under the box shows how many you have used.
3. Check the **Preview**. It shows a sample message with your footer under it, in italics.
4. Select **Save footer**. The page confirms that it applies from the next message you send.

### Sending with no footer

Leave the box blank and save, or select **Remove my footer**. **Remove my footer** is only available when a footer is saved, and it takes effect straight away.

## Plan and access

Editing the footer needs two things:

- A plan that includes the **Remove branding** feature. Without it, the page is read-only. It shows **Current footer (platform default)** and a **See plans** link to [Plans](help:account-plans).
- The permission to edit the outgoing message footer. Without it, you can see the footer but not change it, and the page says only workspace administrators can edit it.

Anyone who can open the page can read the current footer.

## If something looks wrong

- **The box is missing and I only see the current footer.** Your plan or your role does not allow editing. The message under the footer says which one.
- **The footer is too long.** Shorten it to 60 characters. Most emoji count as one character.
- **Saving says you are not allowed.** Your plan or permission may have changed while the page was open. Reload the page to see what you can do now.
- **A template message has no footer.** That is expected, because approved templates keep their own text.
