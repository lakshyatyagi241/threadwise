## What it is for

The Guidebook is a library of step-by-step help articles for connecting, sending and troubleshooting. Every workspace sees the same articles. Use it to find an answer yourself before you contact support.

## How to use it

1. Open **Guidebook** from the Help group of the user menu, or go straight to `/guidebook`.
2. Type a keyword in **Search guidebook articles** and press Enter. The search looks at the title, the short summary and the full text of each article. You can also pick one of the **Popular** suggestions under the box.
3. To narrow the list, choose a category in the **Categories** column. **All articles** shows everything, and every row shows how many articles it holds. Search and a category work together.
4. Each card shows the category, the reading time and a short summary. Select a card or **Read article** to open it.
5. At the bottom of an article, answer **Was this helpful?** with **Yes** or **No**. You get one vote per article, and choosing again replaces your earlier one.
6. Related articles are listed under the article. Use **Back to articles** to return to the list.

## Good to know

- The list shows the matching articles together with their count. If there are many, **Show more articles** loads the next set.
- Only published articles appear. If you open an article that is not available, you are sent back to the list with a notice saying so.
- Reading times are an estimate based on the length of the article.
- **Can't find it? Ask support**, **Contact support** and **Open a ticket** all lead to the Support screen.

## Plan and access

The Guidebook has no plan or role requirement: any signed-in member can open it. Your vote is saved against your own user.

## If something looks wrong

- **No articles match.** Try a different keyword or choose **All articles**.
- **The vote could not be saved.** Select **Yes** or **No** again.
- **An article is missing.** It may be unpublished. Ask support if you expected to find it.
