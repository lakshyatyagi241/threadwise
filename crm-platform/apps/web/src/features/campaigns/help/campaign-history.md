## What it is for

Every campaign you make shows up on one of three pages, depending on how you sent it, so you can find it again without searching one long list.

## How to use it

1. **Campaign history** lists every campaign that has run or is running, newest first. The **Type** column tells you how it went out: **Send now**, **Scheduled**, or **Recurring run** (one run of a recurring campaign). Use the filters for type, status, a date range or a name. Select a row to open its page.
2. **Scheduled campaigns** lists the campaigns waiting for their time, soonest first, with the time in your workspace time zone and how long is left. Select **Cancel campaign** to stop one before it starts.
3. **Recurring campaigns** lists the recurring campaigns with the rule in words, the next run, the last run and how many runs have started. **Pause**, **Resume** or **Cancel series** from the row, and **Show runs** to see each run with its status and counts.

When you launch from the guided flow in **Create campaign**, you land on that campaign's own status page; it then appears on the page that matches how you sent it.

## Options and settings

- You need permission to view campaigns to see these pages, and permission to control campaigns to cancel, pause or resume.
- Times on these pages use the time zone set for your workspace.
- Campaigns made in the classic builder that have already started also appear in **Campaign history**.
- Broadcasts sent before they became campaigns are not lost. When your workspace has any, **Campaign history** shows a **Legacy broadcasts** button that opens the old list, where you can still open each one.

## Troubleshooting

- A campaign you just scheduled is not on page one of **Scheduled campaigns**: the list is ordered by time, so a campaign further away is on a later page. Use the search box.
- **Cancel series** stops future runs and any run that is still waiting or sending. Messages already sent are not affected.
