## What it is for

The **Summary** tab answers the questions an admission head asks after a campaign: how many people we messaged, how many received and opened it, how many wrote back and how many filled in an application form. It uses plain words and counts people, not messages: someone who wrote back three times is one person.

A finished campaign opens on this tab. For a campaign that is still running, open **Summary** from the tabs at the top of the campaign page. On the legacy broadcast page the same summary sits at the top of the **Overview** tab, and on the screen of a launched broadcast it sits under the title.

## How to use it

1. Read the sentence at the top. It adds up the numbers below it, for example how many people received the message and how many wrote back.
2. Look at the five tiles: **Messaged**, **Received**, **Opened**, **Wrote back** and **Filled a form**. Each shows the number of people and what share of the people you messaged that is.
3. Choose how long to wait for a reply: **Within 3 days**, **Within 7 days** or **Within 14 days**. The days are counted from the moment you sent the message. The choice is kept in the address of the page, so a link or a reload shows the same window.
4. Under **Which forms count as an application?** pick the forms that matter. Leave it empty to count every web form and WhatsApp form. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
5. Open **See who** and select **Wrote back**, **Filled a form** or **Not reached** to see the names, phone numbers and times. A name opens the contact. Select **Show more** to load the next page.
6. Select **Print summary** for a one-page A4 print, or **Download names** for a spreadsheet file of the three lists.

## What the numbers mean

- **Wrote back** counts people who sent you a message in the inbox after your message went out and inside the window you chose.
- **Filled a form** counts people who submitted one of the forms you picked, inside the same window. If your role may not read form submissions, this tile and that part of the sentence are left out.
- The line chart shows how many people wrote back on each day, so you can see when replies slow down.

## Plan and access

You need the right to view campaigns (or broadcasts, on the legacy page). The lists of names follow the same right. **Download names** needs the right to export. Form counts need the right to read form submissions.

## If something looks wrong

- **The summary says nothing has been sent.** The campaign has not started sending. The numbers appear once messages go out.
- **A note says some people could not be matched to a contact.** We find replies by phone number, so for those people we cannot tell whether they wrote back. The real number can be higher.
- **A number looks lower than you expect.** People who asked not to be messaged are not counted, and someone who writes from another phone number is not matched to their contact.
- **The numbers could not load.** Select **Try again**. They are worked out when you open the page, and again when you return to the browser tab.
