## What it is for

Settings is where everything you configure lives. The settings are grouped into sections: channels, data and customization, business and billing, team and permissions, automation and AI, and data and security. Each section shows the settings you may change, one tile each.

## How to use it

1. Select **Settings** at the bottom of the menu.
2. Select a section on the left (on a phone, swipe the list of sections) to see its tiles.
3. Select a tile to open that setting. On the setting's page, the breadcrumb links back to Settings.
4. To find a setting quickly, type in **Search settings**. Matching settings from every section appear, and the search looks at their names, keywords and help lines.
5. Press **Escape** or the clear button to return to the sections. Press **/** anywhere on the page to jump to the search box.

> Some pages have a gear button in the top bar. It opens the settings that belong to that page.

## Options and settings

The Settings page has no settings of its own. It shows the tiles you may use.

## Plan and access

The Settings page is open to every signed-in member, but each tile appears only when your role may open it. A section with no tiles you may use is hidden. A tile marked **Upgrade** is part of a higher plan; it opens, but its features need that plan. A tile marked **Soon** is not available yet.

## If something looks wrong

- **A setting is missing.** Your role does not include it. Ask a workspace Owner or Admin if you need it.
- **Search finds nothing.** Try a shorter or broader word, or open the section and browse.
- **A tile shows Upgrade.** Your plan does not include that feature. See [Plans](/account/plans) to compare plans.
- **New to the workspace?** Read [Getting started](help:getting-started) first.
