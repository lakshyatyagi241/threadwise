## What it is for

Notification preferences are yours alone. They decide how each kind of event reaches you: a new chat, a message on a chat you own, a mention, a WhatsApp number that disconnects, a broadcast or campaign that finishes or fails, a message in Team chat, and the tasks, leads, forms and wallet alerts. Your colleagues keep their own choices. Open the page from **Account**, **Notifications**, or from **Notification settings** at the bottom of the bell panel.

## The three switches

Every event has up to three switches:

- **Bell**: the event is added to the bell and counts in its number. Turn it off and the event never reaches your bell.
- **Pop-up**: while this tab is in front, a small pop-up appears with a link to open what happened. It needs the bell to be on.
- **Sound**: a short two-tone ping. It also needs the bell, and only some events can ping (new chats and messages, mentions and Team chat).

A dash in a column means that kind of event does not offer that switch.

## Change a switch

1. Find the event under its heading, for example **Chats** or **Broadcasts and campaigns**.
2. Select a switch. It changes straight away.
3. If saving fails, the switch goes back and a message at the top says so. Try again.

Under each event the page shows the default it follows, for example "Default: bell on, pop-up on, sound off". A switch you have not touched follows that default, so if the default changes later you get the new behaviour. An event you changed is marked **Changed**.

## Go back to the defaults

Choose **Reset to default** on one event to make it follow the default again, or **Reset everything to default** at the top to do it for every event you changed. Both are saved immediately.

## When the workspace turned an event off

If your workspace administrator turned an event off in **Workspace settings**, the row says **Turned off by your workspace** and its switches are off and cannot be changed. Ask an administrator to turn it back on.

## This browser

Under the events, **This browser** has the sound switch and the desktop alerts control. Those two are saved per browser, not per account, and are described in [Notifications and alerts](help:notifications-and-alerts).

## If something looks wrong

- A switch will not move: it is grey because the workspace turned the event off, or because the bell for that event is off. Turn the bell on first.
- The page says it could not load your preferences: select **Try again**. Until it loads, alerts follow each event's default.
- A pop-up you turned off still appeared: reload the page so every tab picks up your latest choices.
