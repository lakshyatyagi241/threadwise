## What it is for

The bell in the header collects what needs your attention: a new chat, new messages on a chat you own, a chat assigned to you, a note that mentions you, a WhatsApp number that connected, disconnected or was logged out, a broadcast, campaign or scheduled message that finished or failed, a Team chat mention or direct message, and tasks, leads and forms. You do not have to be on the Inbox page to find out about a chat: the bell, a pop-up, a sound and the tab title all update on any page, within a few seconds.

## The bell

1. Look at the red number on the bell. It is the number of notifications you have not read.
2. Select the bell to see the newest ones. A chat that keeps writing stays as one row with a count, for example "3 new messages from Ada", and moves to the top each time.
3. Select a row to open what it is about: the conversation, the device, the broadcast, campaign or scheduled message, or the Team chat channel. The row is marked read.
4. Choose **Mark all read** to clear the number.

The bell stays in step across your browser tabs and devices: reading something in one place clears it in the others.

## The pop-up

When something arrives and this tab is in front, a small pop-up appears in the top corner with the contact name or the event, the first words of the message and an **Open** link. A problem, such as a number that disconnected or a send that failed, is drawn in red and stays a little longer. It disappears by itself after a few seconds, or when you press Escape while it is focused or choose the close button.

- A busy chat shows at most one pop-up every few seconds, and a flood of different chats becomes a single "new notifications" pop-up.
- You get no pop-up for the conversation you are reading, or for your own actions.
- Screen readers are told about each new notification once, in a polite announcement that does not move your focus.

## Sound

New chats and messages, mentions and Team chat messages play a short two-tone ping. Your browser only allows sound after you have clicked or pressed a key on the page once. Turn the sound off or on in the footer of the bell panel. The choice is remembered on this browser. With several tabs open, only one of them plays the sound.

## Desktop alerts

When this tab is in the background, a desktop alert can tell you a chat arrived. It shows the contact name only, never the message text, because it may appear on a locked screen.

1. Open the bell and choose **Enable desktop alerts**.
2. Allow notifications when the browser asks. The browser is asked only when you choose this.
3. Use **Desktop alerts: on** in the same place to switch them off again without changing the browser permission.

If the footer says alerts are blocked, allow notifications for this site in your browser settings.

## Choose what reaches you

You decide, event by event, whether it reaches the bell, pops up and plays a sound. Open **Notification settings** at the bottom of the bell panel. See [Notification preferences](help:notification-preferences).

## The browser tab

The tab title shows the number of unread notifications in front of the page name, for example "(3) Team inbox", so you can see it from another tab. It goes away when you have read everything.

## If something looks wrong

- No pop-ups or sound but the number changes after a minute: the live connection is not working, so the bell checks once a minute instead. Reload the page; if it persists, tell your administrator.
- No sound: click once anywhere on the page, then check that the sound is on in the bell footer and that the browser is not muting the tab.
- Notifications for new chats and assignments can be switched off for the whole workspace by an administrator in the notification settings.
