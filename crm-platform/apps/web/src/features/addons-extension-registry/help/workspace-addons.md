## What it is for

Add-ons are extra feature packs you buy on top of your current plan, without changing the plan itself. A pack can switch on a feature your plan does not include, or raise a limit, for example by adding more of something your plan caps. The Add-ons page lists the packs you already own and the packs you can still buy.

## How to use it

1. Open **Marketplace**, stay on the **Integrations** tab, then select **Add-ons** under **Integrations and developer**. You can also go straight to `/addons`.
2. Look at **Your add-ons** at the top, if it is shown. Each row names a pack you own and says when it ends, or **Lifetime** when it never ends. A pack you own is marked **Active**.
3. Browse the cards under **Add-ons**. Each card shows the name, a short subtitle, the price with its period (a number of days, weeks, months or years, or **one-time**), and a tick list of what the pack gives you.
4. Select **Add to plan** on the pack you want. This opens the checkout for that pack, where you pay.
5. When you are done, the pack appears under **Your add-ons** the next time you open the page.

## What the cards tell you

- **Active** with no button means you already own a pack that only unlocks features, so there is nothing more to buy.
- **Buy again** appears on a pack that adds to a limit. It says how many times you own it, and buying it again adds more on top.
- A price shown as **Free** means the pack costs nothing.
- If a pack has an offer price, the card shows the offer price.

## Plan and access

Add-ons need an active paid plan. If your workspace is on a free plan, or your paid plan has run out, the page shows **Add-ons need an active plan** with a **View plans** button that opens [Plans](help:account-plans). Any signed-in member can open the page and read it. The same packs also appear near the bottom of the Plans page. Add-ons that belong to a Marketplace app are not listed here; they are sold on the app's own page in the [Marketplace](help:marketplace-overview).

## If something looks wrong

- **The page says there are no add-ons available.** Nothing is on sale for your workspace right now. Check back later.
- **A pack you bought is missing.** Open the page again after the payment finishes. Only packs that are still running are listed, so an expired pack disappears.
- **The list failed to load.** Use the retry button on the message, or reload the page.
