## What it is for

Add-ons are extra packs you buy on top of a paid plan, without changing the plan itself. There are two kinds, listed under their own headings: **Feature add-ons** switch on a part of the product your plan does not include (for example Smart routing), and **Capacity add-ons** raise a limit (for example an extra seat). Add-ons are monthly or yearly subscriptions. The same list appears on the Add-ons page, on the **Add-ons** tab of the Marketplace and under the plans on the Plans page.

## How to use it

1. Open **Plans** and scroll to **Add-ons**, or open the Add-ons page directly at `/addons`.
2. Look at **Add-ons you own**, if it is shown. Each row says when the add-on renews, or until when it is active if it will not renew, or **Lifetime**.
3. Browse the cards. Each card shows the name, a short subtitle, the price with its period, how it renews, and a tick list of what the pack gives you.
4. Select **Buy add-on** on the pack you want. This opens the checkout for that pack, where you pay.
5. When you are done, the pack appears under **Add-ons you own** the next time you open the page.

## What the cards tell you

Every card is in exactly one state:

- **Included in your plan.** Your plan already gives you everything this add-on does, so there is nothing to buy and no charge. If an account manager switched the features on for your workspace, the card says so. If you were already paying for the add-on, it keeps running until the end of the term you paid for, is not refunded, and then stops renewing; the card shows the end date.
- **Active.** You own it. The card shows when it renews, or until when it runs if it will not renew. A pack that adds to a limit also offers **Buy again**, which adds more on top.
- **Buy add-on.** You can buy it now. The price is the one for your plan: the same add-on can cost differently on different plans. If the add-on has an offer price, the card shows it next to the usual price.
- **Needs an active paid plan.** Your workspace is on a free plan, or your paid plan has ended. A notice at the top says which, and **View plans** opens [Plans](help:account-plans). Renew or choose a paid plan, then come back.

If your plan only gives part of an add-on, the card stays buyable and lists only what you would still get.

Some plans do not offer some add-ons at all. Those are not listed. If you already own one, it stays under **Add-ons you own** until its term ends, but it cannot be bought again on that plan.

## Renewal and payment

Add-ons renew every term. With a card or another online method that supports it, the renewal is paid automatically. With bank transfer or offline payment nothing is charged for you: you pay again when the term ends, and the add-on stops at the end date if you do not. You can stop an automatic renewal from **Add-ons you own**; the add-on keeps working until the end of the term you paid for.

## Who can buy

Everyone in the workspace can see the list. Only people who are allowed to buy for the workspace, usually the owner and admins, can start a purchase. For everyone else the buy button is off and the card says to ask your account owner.

## Message credits are separate

Credits for sending messages are not an add-on. Use **Top up wallet** (the link under the list) to add credits.

## Plan and access

Add-ons need an active paid plan. They do not need the Marketplace feature on your plan: the list is available on every paid plan from the Plans page, while the **Add-ons** tab of the Marketplace needs the Marketplace on your plan like every Marketplace tab. Add-ons that belong to a Marketplace app are not listed here; they are sold on the app's own page in the [Marketplace](help:marketplace-overview).

## If something looks wrong

- **The page says there are no add-ons on sale.** Nothing is on sale for your workspace right now. Check back later.
- **A pack you bought is missing.** Open the page again after the payment finishes. Only packs that are still running are listed, so an expired pack disappears.
- **A card says Included in your plan but I am still charged.** An add-on you already paid for finishes its term first and then stops renewing. Nothing is refunded automatically.
- **The list failed to load.** Use the retry button on the message, or reload the page.
