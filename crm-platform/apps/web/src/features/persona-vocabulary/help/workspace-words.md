## What it is for

The app calls the people you talk to **Contacts**. A clinic says Patients, a studio says Members, a sales team says Leads. **Words we use** lets you rename four things so the screens read the way your team talks: **Contact**, **Appointment**, **Deal** and **Meeting**.

Only the words people read change. Nothing is renamed underneath: your data, exports, imports, integrations, the API and the messages you send to your customers keep their usual names.

## How to rename a word

1. Open **Settings**, then **Words we use** under Business. You need permission to manage workspace settings.
2. Find the noun, for example **Contacts**. The grey text in each box shows the standard word.
3. Type the **singular** (Patient) and the **plural** (Patients). Both are needed; some languages use one word for both and then show a single box.
4. Select **Save**. The menu, the list, the contact page and the Appointments or Deals screens use your words as soon as the page refreshes.

To go back, select **Use standard words** on that noun.

## Languages

You are editing the words for the language the app is in, shown at the top of the page. People using the app in another language keep the standard words there until you set words for that language too.

Tick **Use these words in every language** to save the same words for all 21 app languages. That suits a word that is the same everywhere, such as Lead or Member. For most words you will want to switch the app language and type the local word instead.

## What changes, and what does not

The words appear on the main menu, the Contacts list (title, add button, search, empty state, selection and bulk actions, views), the contact quick view and record page, the Appointments screen and the Deals board.

Some places still use the standard word: Settings pages for contact stages, properties and groups, the Inbox side panel, campaigns, flows and forms, dashboards and reports, notifications, emails and the messages sent to your customers, and these Help guides.

## Limits

- Your words are placed exactly as you type them. The app does not adjust grammar in other languages (gender, cases, articles), so check each language you rename. In English, "a" and "an" follow your word.
- A language that has one word for both appointments and meetings (for example Polish or Russian) cannot show a different name for each, so those two renames are not shown there. They still apply in other languages.
- A name is 1 to 40 characters: letters, numbers, spaces and `- . & / ( )`. Characters that have a special meaning in messages, such as braces, are not allowed.
- If your account team or your business type already set a word, the page says so; what you save here takes priority.

## If something looks wrong

- A screen still says the old word: it is one of the places listed above, or the words were saved for a different language. Check the language shown at the top of the page.
- "These words changed while you were editing": someone else saved first. The latest version is shown; make your change again.
