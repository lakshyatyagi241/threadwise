## What it is for

The product has two sides that work together. The **Chat** side is the inbox, channels, team chat, calling and the tools around them. The **Sales CRM** side is deals, companies, tasks, invoicing, record assignment and the sales dashboard. **Contacts** are shared by both: every workspace keeps its contact list and the contact page.

An edition is simply which sides a workspace has. There are three:

- **Chat**: the conversation side with contacts. The contact page shows the conversation side only, with no deals, tasks or assignment sections.
- **Sales CRM**: the sales side with contacts. The inbox, inbox wording and chat channels are left out of the menu and settings.
- **Both**: everything, the same as the product has always looked.

## How an edition is made

An edition is not a separate product and not a setting called "edition". It comes from two things your account team controls.

1. **The plan** decides what you may use. Each plan feature belongs to a product line (Chat, Sales CRM or Platform, which both sides share). A Chat plan has the Sales CRM features switched off, a Sales CRM plan has the Chat features switched off, and a plan for both has all of them on. The plan editor groups the features by product line so this is a few clicks.
2. **The business type** decides what you see. The "Chat team", "Sales team" and "Chat and sales" business types hide the screens of the other side so people only meet words and menus that apply to them.

Hiding is not locking. A hidden screen is not drawn and shows no upgrade prompt, but nothing is removed: its data and a saved or shared link still work when your plan and role allow it. And a business type never gives access to something the plan does not include; a visible screen the plan lacks still shows its lock.

## For operators

- To build a plan for an edition, open the plan in the operator console, go to **Features** and use **Disable all** under the product line you do not sell. Existing workspaces on other plans are not changed.
- To show a workspace one of the three business types, the platform team attaches it to the workspace. There is no screen for the workspace to choose it yet, and every workspace stays on the generic business type until one is attached.
- Role presets per edition (for example a read-only role for the Chat edition) are not part of this release.

## If something looks wrong

If a workspace cannot find a screen, check two places: the plan (is the feature on?) and the business type (is the screen hidden?). Switching the workspace back to the generic business type shows every screen the plan allows.
