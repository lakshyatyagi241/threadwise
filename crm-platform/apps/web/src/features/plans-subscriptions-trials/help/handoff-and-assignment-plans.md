## What it is for

Handing a conversation from a bot to a person works on every plan. What a plan adds is automation: picking the person for you, and linking the contact's owner to the chat's owner. This guide lists what each level gives, so you know why an option is marked **Not in your plan** on the [Handoff and assignment](help:handoff-settings) page.

## On every plan

- The **Human handoff** step in flows, the AI assistant's handoff and **Send to queue** by a teammate. See [Human handoff](help:flows-human-handoff).
- The **Waiting** tab, **Claim**, assigning by hand, the handoff card and the notification to the team. See [Waiting queue and claiming](help:waiting-queue).
- Sending a conversation to one named person or to one team. A team set to manual keeps it in its queue.
- **Claiming makes you the contact owner**.

## With assignment rules

These need the plan feature that includes conversation auto-assignment:

- The destination **The assignment rules** on a handoff, and automatic assignment of new conversations.
- A team method of **Round robin** or **Least loaded** when a conversation is handed to the team.

Without it, a conversation sent to the rules, or to a team that picks automatically, waits in the queue and says so in its handoff card.

## With contact owner and sticky agent

These need assignment rules **and** the plan feature for contact owner and sticky agent:

- **Owner first** and **Keep in sync** between the contact owner and the chat owner.
- The limit in days for keeping a returning contact with their last agent.
- The question about the contact owner when a chat is reassigned by hand.

## Change what your plan includes

Ask the account owner to change the plan. Nothing is lost when an option is not included: conversations simply wait in the queue until someone claims them.
