## What it is for

Handing a conversation from a bot to a person works on every plan. What a plan adds is automation: picking the person for you, and linking the contact's owner to the chat's owner. This guide lists what each level gives, so you know why an option is marked **Not in your plan** on the [Conversation auto-assignment](help:auto-assignment) page.

## On every plan

The entry plan has no automatic assignment, and everything below works on it:

- The **Human handoff** step in flows, the AI assistant's handoff and **Send to queue** by a teammate. See [Human handoff](help:flows-human-handoff).
- The **Waiting** tab, **Claim**, assigning by hand, the handoff card and the notification to the team. See [Waiting queue and claiming](help:waiting-queue).
- Sending a conversation to one named person or to one team. A team set to **Manual** keeps it in its queue.
- **Return to bot**, **Time off** and **Available / Offline** in the account menu, and the status dots on Team members.
- **Claiming makes you the contact owner**, and the alert to the team when a conversation is waiting.

## With Smart routing

Smart routing is an add-on, or part of a higher plan ([Smart routing add-on](help:smart-routing-addon)). It adds:

- Automatic assignment of new conversations: the Auto Assignment switch, your rules, **Everyone else** and **Try it**.
- The destination **The assignment rules** on a handoff.
- A team method of **Round robin** or **Least loaded**, and the team's default assignee that takes a chat when nobody else can.
- Sticky Agent, **Owner first** and **Keep in sync** between the contact owner and the chat owner, the limit in days for keeping a returning contact with their last agent, and the question about the contact owner when a chat is reassigned by hand.

Without it, a conversation sent to the rules, or to a team that picks automatically, waits in the queue and says so in its handoff card. Each locked option says that Smart routing unlocks it and links to Plans and add-ons.

## Change what your plan includes

Ask the account owner to add the Smart routing add-on or change the plan. Nothing is lost when an option is not included or stops being included: your saved rules and settings are kept, chats already waiting or assigned stay where they are, and conversations simply wait in the queue until someone claims them.
