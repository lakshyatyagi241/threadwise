## What your plan includes

Appointment reminders live in the **Appointments** app. These come with the app on every plan that has it:

- Timed reminders on WhatsApp and email: the booking confirmation, 24 hours, 2 hours and 15 minutes before (online appointments), the after-appointment message and the no-show message, up to 5 steps with a time per booking type.
- The seven ready-made sequences, in English and Hindi.
- The email fallback when WhatsApp cannot deliver, the test email, the reminder report and the history on each appointment.

Two parts are the **upgrade tier** and need a plan that includes them. Nothing else about reminders changes with it.

- **Confirm, Reschedule and Cancel buttons**: three quick-reply buttons under a WhatsApp reminder sent before the appointment.
- **Rebook messages**: a message sent some days after a completed visit, asking the contact to book again.

To see whether your plan has them, open **Settings**, then **Appointment settings**, then **Reminders and follow-ups**: a part your plan lacks is shown locked, with a line that says so and a link to the plans. Subscribing to the Appointments app alone does not add them; they come with the plan. Ask your administrator or the platform operator to switch to a plan that includes them.

For the reminders themselves, see [Reminders and follow-ups](help:appointments-reminders).

## Confirm, Reschedule and Cancel buttons

**What the customer sees.** The reminder arrives on WhatsApp with three buttons. Each tap does one thing:

- **Confirm** marks the appointment as confirmed by the customer and sends a short reply in the chat. A reminder set to skip when the customer already confirmed is not sent.
- **Cancel** cancels the appointment, as if the customer had cancelled on their booking page. Reminders still waiting stop, and the host is notified.
- **Reschedule** replies with the link to choose a new time.

A second tap on the same button gets the same reply and does nothing more. After the appointment has started, a tap gets a reply that the reminder is no longer active.

**Where to set it.** Open Settings, then Appointment settings, then Reminders and follow-ups, and open the booking type. On a reminder before the appointment, switch on **Add Confirm, Reschedule and Cancel buttons (WhatsApp)**. The switch needs WhatsApp to be one of the reminder's channels, and the WhatsApp template you choose must have quick-reply buttons. A ready-made sequence sets the buttons up when your plan includes them.

**If your plan does not include it.** The switch is locked, with a line that says the buttons are part of the Appointments upgrade tier and an **Upgrade your plan** link. A ready-made sequence is added without the buttons, and the result says so. A reminder that already had them is kept, but it is sent without working buttons until your plan includes them again. You can switch them off. Nothing you set up is deleted. The reminder report leaves out the button columns, with the same note.

## Rebook messages

**What the customer sees.** A message on WhatsApp or email, a set number of days after a visit that was marked completed, asking them to book again. It is not sent if the contact already has a later booking. It counts as marketing, so the marketing consent rules apply.

**Where to set it.** In the reminder timeline of the booking type, select **Add step**, then **Rebook message, some days later**. Set the number of days after the visit (30 by default, up to a year), the channels and the message, then switch the step **On**. A new step starts off.

**If your plan does not include it.** You can still add and set up the step, but its **On** switch is locked, with a line that says it is part of the Appointments upgrade tier and an **Upgrade your plan** link. Saving it switched on is refused. A rebook step that was already on is kept but never sent, and the appointment shows it as Not sent with the reason. Ready-made sequences save the rebook step off. When your plan includes rebook messages again, the same step sends again.

## If something looks wrong

- **The buttons are not on the reminder.** Check that your plan includes them, that the reminder is before the appointment, that WhatsApp is one of its channels, and that the template has quick-reply buttons.
- **A rebook message was not sent.** Check that your plan includes rebook messages, that the visit was marked completed, that the contact has no later booking, that the contact's consent allows marketing where your workspace asks for it, and that the step is On.
- **Reminders changed after we changed plan.** Only the two upgrade-tier parts stop. Timed reminders keep going.
