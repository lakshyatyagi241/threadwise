## What it is for

Routing rules decide who receives a new conversation, based on conditions such as the channel or the topic.

## How to use it

1. Open the inbox routing settings and create a rule.
2. Set the conditions and the person or team the rule assigns to.
3. Order the rules, since the first match wins.

## Plan and access

Routing rules are included in plans that list them. On a plan without them, the routing settings are locked. Managing rules needs the inbox settings permission.

## If something looks wrong

A conversation that goes to the wrong person usually matches an earlier rule. Check the order of the rules and their conditions.
