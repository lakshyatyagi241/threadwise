## What it is for

The WooCommerce integration connects your WooCommerce store, so its products, orders and automations can be seen and managed from here.

## How to use it

1. Open the WooCommerce store from the Store area of the menu.
2. Connect the store with its address and keys.
3. Review the products and orders the store sends.

## Plan and access

The WooCommerce integration is included in plans that list it. On a plan without it, the store screen is locked. Connecting needs the store permission for your role.

## If something looks wrong

A store that does not sync usually has wrong or revoked keys. Enter the keys again from the store settings.
