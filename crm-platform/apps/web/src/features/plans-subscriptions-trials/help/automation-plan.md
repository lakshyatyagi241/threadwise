## What it is for

Automation lets your workspace run flows and rules by itself: a reply when a keyword arrives, or a follow-up that waits a set time.

## How to use it

1. Open the Automation area of the menu and choose the screen you need.
2. Create or open a flow or rule, then save it and turn it on.
3. Check its recent runs to confirm it works as you expect.

## Plan and access

Automation is included in plans that list it. On a plan without it, the automation screens are locked and the menu shows an upgrade mark. Your role also needs permission to manage automations.

> You can compare plans in [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).

## If something looks wrong

A flow that does not start is usually switched off or has no start step. Open it, check its status, then review its last runs for an error.
