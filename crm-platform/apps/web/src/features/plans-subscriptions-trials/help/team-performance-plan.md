## What it is for

Team performance shows how the team handles conversations, such as response times and the number of open chats per person.

## How to use it

1. Open the team performance screen from the menu or the analytics area.
2. Pick the period you want to review.
3. Compare people or teams, and open a person to see their conversations.

## Plan and access

Team performance is included in plans that list it. On a plan without it, the screen is locked. Viewing it needs the report permission for your role.

## If something looks wrong

Numbers that look empty usually cover a period with no handled conversations. Change the period and check again.
