## What it is for

Remove branding hides the product's own branding on pages that customers see, such as booking and public pages, so they show your business instead.

## How to use it

1. Open the branding settings under Settings.
2. Turn off the product branding option and save.
3. Open a public page to check that the branding is gone.

## Plan and access

Removing branding is included in plans that list it. On a plan without it, the option is locked in the branding settings.

## If something looks wrong

Branding that still shows may be cached on a page you already opened. Reload the page, then check again.
