## What it is for

Plan and usage shows what your workspace is on, when the plan is valid until, and what it has used against each limit. It also lists the features your plan includes, and which ones need a higher plan.

## How to use it

1. Open your account menu and select **Plan and usage**, or go to [Plan and usage](/account/usage).
2. Read the **Current plan** card. Its badge shows the state: Free trial, Active, Lapsed, Free, Custom or Trial ended.
3. Read the sentence under it. It says when a trial or paid plan ends, or that the plan has no end date.
4. Under **Usage and limits**, each bar shows how much of a limit you have used. A bar turns red at 90% and above. Some limits count this month only; others count in total.
5. Under **What your plan includes**, a tick means the feature is on your plan. Select **Compare plans** to see the plans that include more.

To change plan, select **Upgrade plan**. It opens [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).

## Options and settings

This page has no settings. Limits and included features are set by the platform for your plan.

## Plan and access

Any signed-in member can open this page. Changing the plan is a separate permission, used on the plans page.

## If something looks wrong

- **A limit shows no maximum.** That limit is unlimited on your plan; the bar only shows usage.
- **The plan says it has lapsed.** The paid period has ended, and paid features are paused until you renew.
- **Usage numbers look out of date.** Reload the page. Monthly allowances reset on the date shown.
- **The page shows an error.** Select **Try again**, and check your connection if it keeps failing.
