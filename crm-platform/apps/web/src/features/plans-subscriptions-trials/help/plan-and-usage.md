## What it is for

Plan and usage shows what your workspace is on, when the plan is valid until, and what it has used against each limit. It also lists the features your plan includes, which ones need a higher plan, and the Marketplace add-ons your plan makes available.

## How to use it

1. Open your account menu and select **Plan and usage**, or go to [Plan and usage](/account/usage).
2. Read the **Current plan** card. Its badge shows the state: Free trial, Active, Lapsed, Free, Custom or Trial ended.
3. Read the sentence under it. It says when a trial or paid plan ends, or that the plan has no end date.
4. Under **Usage and limits**, limits are grouped: Messaging, Workspace, Content, Sales, Integrations and AI. Groups with a maximum start open; the others are collapsed, and you can open or close any group by selecting its name.
5. Each bar shows how much of a limit you have used. It changes colour as it fills: a warning colour from 80% and red when the limit is full. Some limits count this month only; others count in total.
6. Limits with no maximum are hidden to keep the page short. Select **Show N unlimited limits** to list them as well, and **Hide unlimited limits** to tuck them away again.
7. Under **What your plan includes**, a tick means the feature is on your plan. Select **Compare plans** to see the plans that include more.
8. Under **Add-ons available on your plan**, each card names a Marketplace app, its price and how often it is billed. **Owned** means your workspace already has it. Otherwise select **Buy** to open the app's page in the Marketplace and subscribe there. If your plan offers no add-ons, this section is not shown.

To change plan, select **Upgrade plan**. It opens [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).

## What the limits count

Each bar counts what the workspace holds now, so deleting something frees its place. Two things are not given back: campaigns created in the plan period, and the invoices created this month. Monthly bars (messages, appointments, invoices, AI tokens) reset on the date shown. Broadcast recipients is the largest audience one broadcast may be sent to, not a running total.

When a limit is full, creating one more is refused with a message that names the limit and links to [Plans and pricing](/account/plans). A limit with no maximum is unlimited on your plan. The one exception is API requests per minute: with no maximum set it shows **Platform default**, meaning the platform's standard rate applies, and it is not unlimited. If a limit shows **No usage tracked**, the page has no counter for it yet.

## Options and settings

This page has no settings. Limits and included features are set by the platform for your plan.

## Plan limits and add-ons on your plan

The bars show the limits that apply to your workspace, which can include values the platform team set for you specifically. On [Plans and pricing](/account/plans), **Compare plans** lists the same limits, features and add-ons side by side. The first column stays in place while you scroll sideways, your plan is tinted, and **Only show differences** hides the rows every plan shares. A plan card shows its main limits and features; select **Show all** to see the rest.

An add-on listed on your plan is available to buy, not already switched on. It starts working once you have bought it.

## Plan and access

Any signed-in member can open this page. Changing the plan is a separate permission, used on the plans page.

## If something looks wrong

- **A limit shows no maximum.** That limit is unlimited on your plan; the bar only shows usage. Turn on **Show N unlimited limits** to see it.
- **An add-on is missing.** Your plan only lists the add-ons the platform team made available for it. Compare plans to see which plans offer more.
- **The plan says it has lapsed.** The paid period has ended, and paid features are paused until you renew.
- **Usage numbers look out of date.** Reload the page. Monthly allowances reset on the date shown.
- **The page shows an error.** Select **Try again**, and check your connection if it keeps failing.
