## What it is for

The Shopify integration connects your Shopify store, so its products, orders and automations can be seen and managed from here.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Integrations**, and select **Shopify**.
2. Connect the store and approve access.
3. Review the products and orders the store sends.

## Plan and access

The Shopify integration is included in plans that list it. On a plan without it, the store screen is locked. Connecting needs the store permission for your role.

## If something looks wrong

A store that does not sync usually has an expired connection. Reconnect the store from its screen.
