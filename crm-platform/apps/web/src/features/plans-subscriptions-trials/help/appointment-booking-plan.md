## What it is for

Appointment booking lets customers choose a free slot and book with your team, through booking pages you set up.

## How to use it

1. Open Appointments and create a booking type with its duration and availability.
2. Share the booking link with customers.
3. Confirm or manage bookings from the appointments screen.

## Plan and access

Appointment booking is included in plans that list it. On a plan without it, the appointments screens are locked and the menu shows an upgrade mark.

## If something looks wrong

A slot that is missing is usually outside the availability you set, or already booked. Check the booking type's availability.
