## What it is for

The kanban view shows conversations as cards on a board, grouped by stage, so the team can move work along visually.

## How to use it

1. Open the inbox and switch to the kanban view.
2. Drag a card to another stage to move the conversation.
3. Open a card to read or reply to the conversation.

## Plan and access

The kanban view is included in plans that list it. On a plan without it, the view is locked and the inbox keeps its list view.

## If something looks wrong

A card that does not move may not have permission to change its stage. Ask a workspace admin to check your role.
