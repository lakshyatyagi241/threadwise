## What it is for

SLA policies set response-time targets for conversations, so the team can see which chats are at risk of waiting too long.

## How to use it

1. Open the inbox settings and create a policy with a first-response target.
2. Choose which conversations the policy applies to.
3. Watch the inbox for conversations flagged as at risk or overdue.

## Plan and access

SLA policies are included in plans that list them. On a plan without them, the policy settings are locked. Managing policies needs the inbox settings permission.

## If something looks wrong

A conversation that is not flagged usually falls outside the policy's conditions. Check which conversations the policy applies to.
