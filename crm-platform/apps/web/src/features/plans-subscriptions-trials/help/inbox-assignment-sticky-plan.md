## What it is for

A returning customer is easier to help when the same person answers. This plan feature adds the owner modes and the continuity window to the auto-assignment settings: Owner first, Keep in sync, and how many days back "the agent they spoke to last" still counts.

## How to use it

1. Open the auto-assignment settings.
2. Switch on Sticky Agent and, under Advanced, pick the owner mode and the window in days.
3. Check the order shown on the page: the contact's own agent comes first, then your rules, then Everyone else.

## Plan and access

The owner modes and the window are part of **Smart routing** ([Smart routing add-on](help:smart-routing-addon)): included in some plans or added with the add-on. On a plan without them, those controls are locked and say so, with a link to Plans, and your saved choices are kept but not applied. Changing the settings needs the inbox settings permission. Auto assignment itself is the other half of Smart routing.

## If something looks wrong

A chat that did not go back to the usual agent usually means that person is away, offline, at their chat limit, or past the window in days. A conversation already assigned by a flow, a person or a handoff is never changed by these settings.
