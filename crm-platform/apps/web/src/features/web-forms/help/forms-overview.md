## What it is for

Forms lets you build a form, put it on a web page of its own or on your website, and have every answer land in your CRM as a contact (and a company, when the form asks for one). Each form has a status: **Draft**, **Published**, **Paused** or **Archived**. Only a published form is visible to the public.

Forms is a paid app. The **Forms** entry appears in the menu, under Campaigns, only for workspaces that have it.

## How to use it

1. Open **Forms** in the menu. The list shows every form with its status, submissions, views, conversion and last submission.
2. Select **New form**. Start from a template (lead capture, contact, event, order, survey) or from a blank form, and give it a name. The form is created as a draft.
3. Build it, style it, choose what happens after it is sent, then select **Publish**. See [Building a form](help:forms-builder).
4. Share the link or paste the embed code on your site. See [Sharing and embedding](help:forms-share-embed).
5. Read the answers under **Submissions**. See [Form submissions](help:forms-submissions).

## The list

- Use the views above the table to show **All**, **Published**, **Drafts**, **Paused** or **Archived** forms, and the search box to find one by name.
- **Conversion** is submissions as a share of views. It stays empty until the form has had a view.
- A form that shows **Unpublished changes** has a draft that differs from the version visitors see.
- The row menu offers **Edit**, **Submissions**, **Copy link**, **Embed on a site**, **Duplicate**, **Pause** or **Resume**, **Archive**, and **Delete** for drafts and archived forms. A paused form tells visitors it is closed. An archived form answers as if it did not exist, and its submissions stay readable.
- **Templates** opens the gallery on its own page.

## Plan and access

Forms needs the Forms app. Seeing forms needs permission to view forms, creating needs the create permission, and changing, publishing, pausing and archiving need the update permission. Reading answers is a separate permission. Your plan limits how many active forms you can have and how many submissions you accept each month. Archiving a form frees its place under the form limit.

## If something looks wrong

- **I cannot create another form.** You have reached the number of forms your plan allows. Archive a form you no longer use, or upgrade.
- **The link says the form is not available.** It is a draft, archived, or the link was rotated. Publish it, or copy the current link from the Share tab.
- **A name is refused.** Another form already uses it. Names are compared without regard to capital letters.
