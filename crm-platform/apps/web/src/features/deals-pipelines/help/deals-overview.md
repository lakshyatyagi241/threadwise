## What it is for

**Deals** shows your sales opportunities for one pipeline, as a board with a column per stage or as a table. Four tiles above the board give the totals for the whole pipeline: **Open pipeline**, **Weighted forecast**, **Won this month** and **Win rate**.

## How to use it

1. Open **Deals** in the CRM area of the menu, or go to `/deals`. Choose a pipeline in the **Pipeline** dropdown if you have more than one.
2. Select **New deal** and fill in the title, value, currency, stage, owner, contact, company and expected close date. Leave **Company** empty to use the contact's company. If you can read companies, you can search for one or create it from the typed name.
3. Move a deal by dragging its card to another column, or use **Move to** in its "..." menu.
4. Moving a deal to a lost stage asks why it was lost.
5. Select a card to open its panel. Fields save when you leave them. Add a contact, change the company, add notes, calls and tasks, or use **Mark won**, **Mark lost** or **Reopen deal**.
6. Narrow the board with the search box, **Owner** and **Source**. Select **Clear** to remove filters.

## Options and settings

- The **Board** and **List** switch changes the view. The list is newest first, with **Load more**.
- **Reports** opens the pipeline reports: value by stage, won and lost per month, top performers and lost reasons.
- The gear opens **Pipeline settings**, see [Pipelines and stages](help:deal-pipelines).

## Plan and access

Opening the screen needs permission to read deals. Creating, moving and deleting need their own permissions. **Reports** needs the report permission and the gear needs permission to edit pipelines; each is hidden without it. Deals belong to the sales pipeline feature of your plan.

## If something looks wrong

- **No pipeline yet.** Create one in **Pipeline settings**.
- **A move failed.** The card goes back and a message says why.
- **Someone else changed this deal.** Reload and try again.
- **Mark as lost will not continue.** Choose a reason first.
