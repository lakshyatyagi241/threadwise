## What it is for

Every contact is in exactly one lifecycle stage, which shows where the person is in your sales journey. A status is a step inside a stage, such as Quote sent while Qualified. This screen controls the stages and statuses your team can choose, in the order they are offered, on the contact form, the contacts table and the filters.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Data and customization**, then **Contact stages**, or go to `/contact-stages`. The two tabs are **Lifecycle stages** and **Statuses**.
2. A workspace without its own list uses the platform's standard stages and statuses. Select **Customise the standard stages** (or **Customise the standard statuses**) to copy them in and edit them, or **Start with my own**.
3. Select **Add stage** or **Add status**, enter a name and pick a colour.
4. Reorder rows by dragging them or with the arrows. The star makes a stage the default, so new contacts start there.
5. Use the pencil to edit a row. Turn **Available** off to hide an option from the pickers; contacts that already have it keep it.
6. Use the bin to delete a row. If contacts use it, you choose where to move them.

## Options and settings

- A stage can mean **Normal**, **Won** or **Lost**. Won and lost stages record the date a contact enters them, and a lost stage asks for a reason.
- A status can apply to **All stages** or to one stage, and a contact only offers the statuses that fit its stage. A stage can have one default status.
- When you move a contact to another stage, its status is reset to that stage's default, or cleared when there is none. The screen says what happened.
- Each row shows how many contacts use it. The **Live preview** shows how your options look in the pickers and the contacts table.
- Your own contact fields are managed in [Contact properties](help:contact-properties).

## Plan and access

Opening the screen needs permission to read contact properties. Adding needs the create permission, editing, reordering and setting defaults need update, and deleting needs delete. Without update you can view the options but not change them. This screen has no plan requirement of its own.

## If something looks wrong

- **Some contacts still hold this value.** Choose where to move them before deleting it. If it is the only option, add another one first.
- **This is the default stage.** Pick another default before hiding it, or new contacts start in the standard first stage.
- **A status is missing for a contact.** It is limited to a different stage.
- **Someone else changed this list.** It was refreshed. Try again.
