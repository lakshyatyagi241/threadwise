## What it is for

The **quick view** shows one contact in a panel on the right, about 60% of the window, while the **Contacts** list stays visible and dimmed behind it. Use it to check a person, make a small change or log something and move to the next contact, without losing your place in the list.

## How to use it

1. In **Contacts**, select a contact's name. The address gains `?contact=` and the contact's id, so you can copy the link and share it.
2. Use the arrow buttons next to **See all details**, or the arrow keys on your keyboard, to move to the previous or next contact of the page you are on. At the edge of the page the arrow is off; go to the next page in the list and open a contact there.
3. Press Esc, select the cross, or select the dimmed list to close it. The browser's Back button closes it too.
4. Select **See all details** to open the contact's [full page](help:contact-details-page).

## What is in the panel

- **The top.** The contact's name, their social links (shown when the contact has Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X address properties), the job title, and the lifecycle stage. Select the job title to type it in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels). It uses your workspace's **Job title** contact property (key `job_title`); if you have no such property, the line is not shown. Select the stage chip to move the contact; a lost stage asks why first.
- **The action bar.** **Note**, **Task**, **Meeting** and **Add company** are shortcuts: nothing opens in a pop-up. Each switches the panel to the tab that holds that form (Notes, Tasks, Meetings or Overview) and puts the cursor in it. The round icons message or call the contact on WhatsApp, email, chat and phone. The **...** menu edits or deletes the contact. Each button is shown only if you have the permission, and a channel the contact cannot be reached on is greyed out with the reason.
- **The tabs.** Overview, Activity (the timeline), Notes, Tasks, Meetings and Files, then a **+3 more** menu with Conversations, Communication stats and Properties. Notes, Tasks and Meetings carry a small count. Arrow keys, Home and End move between the tabs. Each opens in the panel; **Open on the details page** takes you to the same section full size.
- **Tasks.** The open tasks of the contact, soonest first. Select **Add task** and a row opens in the list: a title, a due date (tomorrow at 17:00 to begin with), who it is for (you to begin with) and an optional priority. Press Enter to save and Esc to close the row; the row stays open so you can add the next one. Tick the circle to complete a task or untick it to reopen it; it changes at once, and goes back with a message if the server refuses. **Show completed** lists the done ones and **View all** opens them in the Tasks hub.
- **Notes.** Write in the box at the top and press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) or **Save note**.
- **Meetings.** Select **Schedule meeting** to open a row in the list: a title, a start, a duration (15 minutes to 2 hours, no end time, or your own end) and an optional link or place. Upcoming meetings come first, then past ones and those with no date; a link opens in a new tab. **Edit** opens the same row in place; **Delete** asks you to confirm.
- **Company.** On the Overview, the **Company** card has a search box: pick a company, or type a new name and choose **Create** if you may create companies. Select the cross to remove the link. It saves at once and goes back if the server refuses.
- **Overview.** Tabs hold the contact's information, social media (only when there are links), lead source (where the contact came from and the first and latest lead touch) and custom fields. **Contact Field History** lists every change made to the contact. On the right: the **Lead score** ring (select **Score details** for the AI score, the reasons and the history), **Subscription status** (WhatsApp, email and SMS as Yes or No), **Owner and assignment** (the owner, the tags with **Add tag**, the groups), **Location**, and **Privacy**.

## Good to know

- **Subscription status.** Yes means the contact is subscribed on that channel. No in red means the contact opted out of marketing messages. The proof is on the contact's **Consent** page.
- **Files.** The list shows the files attached to the contact. Uploading is not available yet.
- **Web analytics** is not shown: no visit or page-view data is kept for a contact.
- **Conversations and communication stats** count the chats found for this contact on your channels and, with the right access, their calls and open tasks. They need access to the Team inbox (and to calls) and are not shown without it.
- **Plan and access.** Reading needs permission to read contacts. Notes, meetings, tags, owner and company changes need permission to update contacts; the Tasks tab needs permission to read tasks and adding one needs permission to create tasks; messaging needs the inbox reply permission and calling needs the calling permission.

## If something looks wrong

- **The arrows are off.** The contact is not on the page you are looking at (you opened it from a link), or it is the first or last row of the page.
- **A tab is missing.** Your role does not include it (Tasks and Conversations need their own access).
- **Someone else changed this contact.** Reload it and apply your change again.
