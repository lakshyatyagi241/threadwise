## What it is for

A follow-up is the next time someone on your team should get back to a contact. Each contact has at most one open follow-up, shown as **Next follow-up** in its header. It is a normal task of kind follow-up, so it appears in your task list and you are reminded when it is due. Nothing happens automatically until you switch an option on here.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Automation and AI**, then **Follow-ups**, or choose the gear on the Contacts page. Only people who manage the workspace settings see this page.
2. Under **Automatically set follow-ups**, switch on the situations where contacting a person should update their follow-up: the contact has no follow-up (a follow-up without a date counts as none), the follow-up is overdue, due today, or due later.
3. Under **Default follow-up date**, choose where the follow-up goes: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week or 1 month later, a custom number of days, **Someday** (no date, it is never reminded) or **No follow-up** (the open follow-up is marked done and no new one is made).
4. Under **Default follow-up time**, choose the alert time. The day and the time are read on your workspace's time zone.
5. Select **Save**.

## Working with a follow-up

- A contact action that counts as getting in touch (a contact button, a message from the inbox, a campaign, broadcast or flow send, a note or other timeline activity, or a logged call) moves the follow-up when one of your options matches. The follow-up belongs to the contact's owner, or to the person who got in touch when there is no owner.
- On a contact with a follow-up, **Done** completes it, **Snooze** moves it to 1 day, 3 days or next week at your default alert time, and **Change date** sets any date and time or **Someday**.
- To give a contact its first follow-up, or to remove one, use the contact's **More** menu: **Set follow-up** and **Remove follow-up**.
- The Contacts list has a **Next follow-up** column and the views **Follow-up today**, **Follow-up overdue** and **Someday follow-ups**, offered once any contact has a follow-up. The filter **Follow-up** finds contacts by state.

## Options and settings

- All four situations start switched off, because automatic follow-ups create tasks and notifications. The default date starts at 3 days, the time at 9:00 AM.
- A contact's own future follow-up is replaced only when **due later** is on; that is your explicit choice.
- Contacting a person twice in a day writes the same date, so nothing is duplicated.

## Plan and access

The page needs permission to manage workspace settings, which Owners and Admins have. Setting, snoozing or completing a follow-up needs permission to edit the contact, and a new follow-up also needs permission to create tasks. This page has no plan requirement of its own.

## If something looks wrong

- **The follow-up did not move.** Check that the situation it was in (none, overdue, today, later) is switched on, and that the action was one that counts.
- **No reminder arrived.** A **Someday** follow-up has no date and is never reminded.
- **Someone else changed these settings.** The page was refreshed with their values. Make your change again.
