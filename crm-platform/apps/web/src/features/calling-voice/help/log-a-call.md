## What it is for

Not every customer call happens on WhatsApp. A call you made from your own phone, or took on the office line, would otherwise leave no trace. **Log a call** writes it down: who it was with, whether it was inbound or outbound, how it went, how long it lasted and what was said. The call then appears in **Call logs** with a **Source** label, and on the contact's timeline when the number belongs to a contact.

Logging records what happened. It is never blocked by consent settings or by the Do-Not-Call list: those decide whether you may place a marketing call, not whether you may write down a call you already had.

## How to use it

1. Open **Call logs** and select **Log a call**. The button shows only if you have the right to log calls.
2. Type the customer's **Phone number**. If a contact you may see has that number, the call is linked to them. If nobody has it, the call is still saved, by its number, and no contact is created.
3. Choose the **Direction** (Inbound is a call to you, Outbound a call from you) and **How it went**: Connected, No answer, Busy, Left a voicemail, Wrong number, Declined or Failed.
4. Set **When it started**. It starts at the current time; change it for a call that was earlier. A call cannot start in the future or more than 400 days ago.
5. Fill in **Length in minutes** if you know it. Leave it empty for an unknown length: a connected call with no length still counts as answered.
6. Add **Notes** if you want (up to 2,000 characters). Anyone who can see the call, and anyone who can see the contact's timeline, can read them (the timeline shows the first words), so keep them to what the team needs.
7. To follow up, tick **Also make a follow-up task** and choose the date. The task is assigned to you and linked to the contact, so it needs a number that belongs to one of your contacts.
8. Select **Save call**.

## Where it shows

- **Call logs**: logged calls are listed with WhatsApp calls. Choose a **Source** in the filter to see one kind only: WhatsApp, Logged by hand, Phone line, Mobile app or Click to call. A logged call's detail shows its notes.
- **The contact's timeline**: one line per call, such as "Outbound call, Connected, 4 min, by Priya", with the first words of the notes under it.
- The totals and the Analytics tab count both kinds. A logged call that connected is **Answered**; an inbound call that did not connect is **Missed**.

## Limits and rights

- You need the right to log calls to see **Log a call**. To see the call afterwards you also need the right to view the call log, and its scope decides whose calls you see (all, your team's, or only your own).
- A call logged against a contact you are not allowed to see is saved by its number alone, and the contact is not shown to you.
- Calls reported by a phone or a telephony provider cannot be edited by the people on the team. They are a record of what happened.
- You do not need WhatsApp calling to log a call or to see Call logs. Without it, Call logs shows the calls you wrote down or that a phone or provider reported.

## If something looks wrong

- **The call is saved but has no contact.** No contact you may see has that number. Add the contact or fix their number, then log the call again if you want it linked.
- **"A follow-up task needs a contact".** The number did not match a contact. Use the number saved on the contact, then try again.
- **"A call cannot start in the future".** Check the date and time; the call must already have started.
- **I cannot find Log a call.** Your role may not have the right to log calls. Ask an owner or admin.
