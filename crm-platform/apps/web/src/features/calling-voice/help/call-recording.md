## What it is for

A call recording is a customer's voice. Recording is **off** until an owner or admin turns it on, and even then a call is recorded only after the person has heard a notice and agreed. How long recordings are kept is yours to set, and old ones are deleted for you.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Calling** under Channels, and scroll to **Call recording**.
2. Write the **Recording notice**: the sentence your team reads out before recording. Select **Use suggested text** if you want a starting point. Every change is saved as a new version, and each call keeps the version that was read to the person.
3. Choose **How the person agrees**. **The person says yes** is the safest: they say yes, and your teammate presses the button that records it. **Announcement, then continue** only records that the notice was read and the call went on. Use it only where your legal advisor has confirmed that an announcement is enough.
4. Set how many days recordings are kept (7 to 365, 90 by default), switch **Record calls** on, and select **Save rules**.
5. During a call, a teammate selects **Record**, reads the notice and presses **They said yes: start recording**. If the person does not agree, press **No: continue without recording**. The call goes on and nothing is kept.

## Options and settings

- **Recording and consent** on a call in [Call logs](help:call-logs-overview) shows the answer, how it was given, when, and which notice version was read.
- **Delete recording** removes the audio for good and keeps the call in the log. It needs the right to manage call recordings.
- **Place legal hold** keeps one recording out of the nightly clean-up and blocks deleting it until the hold is lifted. A reason is required, and placing or lifting a hold is written to the audit trail. It needs the right to manage data requests.
- If a person asks later for their recording to be deleted, use **Delete recording**, or handle it as a data request so every copy of their data is covered.

> The notice texts and the choice of announcement or a spoken yes are legal decisions for your business. This screen records what happened; it does not tell you what the law in your country requires.

## Plan and access

Everyone who takes calls can read the rules and record the person's answer. Changing the rules and deleting a recording need the right to manage call recordings, held by owners and admins by default. The page needs the **WhatsApp calling** plan feature.

## If something looks wrong

- **Record says recording is off.** An owner or admin has to turn on **Record calls** first, after writing a notice.
- **A recording was not kept.** The person did not agree, the answer could not be saved, or the browser could not record. Nothing is stored without a saved answer.
- **A recording is gone.** Open the call: the card shows when it was deleted and why, for example that the keeping period ended.
- **Delete is greyed out.** The recording is on legal hold. Lift the hold first.
