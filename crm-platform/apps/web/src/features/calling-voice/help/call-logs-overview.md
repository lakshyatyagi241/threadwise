## What it is for

Call logs lists every WhatsApp call to or from your numbers, with its length, who handled it and how it ended. It also lists the calls that were not on WhatsApp and were written down with [Log a call](help:log-a-call); pick a **Source** in the filter to see one kind only. Open a call to see what happened step by step and, if it was recorded, to play the recording. The Analytics tab turns the same calls into figures.

## How to use it

1. Open **Call logs** in the menu. The newest calls come first, 25 to a page.
2. Pick a time range on the left: **Last 24h**, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**. For other days, fill in **From** and **To** under Custom range and select **Apply**.
3. Narrow the list with **Status** on the left, or with the search bar: type part of a phone number or a contact name, and choose a direction, a number or the teammate who handled the call. Select **Filter**.
4. Select a call's name or **Open** to see its detail. **Open contact** and **Open in inbox** take you to the person and to the conversation.
5. To see who agreed to the recording, when, and when it will be deleted, look at the **Recording and consent** card on the call. To listen, select **Play recording** on the call. The player works for a few minutes. If the link has run out, press play again and a new one is made.

The filters are kept in the address of the page, so you can bookmark a view or send it to a teammate, and a reload keeps it.

## What the numbers mean

- The four boxes at the top (Total, Last 24h, Completed, Failed) count every call you can see, whatever the filters say. Failed also counts calls nobody answered.
- The row under them, **In this selection**, follows your filters. **Answered** is a call that ended with talk time. **Missed** is a call a customer made that ended with no talk time. A call you placed that nobody picked up is **No answer** but is not counted as missed.
- **Average length** is the average talk time of the answered calls.

## Analytics

Select **Analytics** to see the calls of the chosen range as: the share answered, the average handle time (talk time of answered calls), calls per day, calls per teammate, and a grid of the busiest hours of the week. Days and hours are counted in your own time zone. Choose a number on the left to look at one line only.

## Who sees what

- You need the right to view the call log. Its record scope decides whose calls you see: all calls, your team's, or only the ones you handled. A call outside your scope is not shown, and its address answers as if it did not exist.
- Recordings are deleted automatically when the keeping period you set in [Recording calls](help:call-recording) ends. An owner or admin can also delete one recording by hand, and the call stays in the log.
- Playing a recording needs its own right. Every time someone presses **Play recording**, it is written to the audit trail with their name.
- Call logs follows your plan: it needs the **WhatsApp calling** plan feature, like placing calls. On a plan without it the menu entry shows a lock.

> A recording is a customer's voice. Check the rules for recording calls where you and your customers are before you keep or share one.

## If something looks wrong

- **A call is missing.** It may be older than the time range, outside your scope, or hidden by a filter. Select **Clear** and widen the range.
- **A call still shows as Live now.** The list updates by itself when a call ends. If WhatsApp never reported the end, reload the page after a few minutes.
- **No Play button.** The call has no recording, or you can see the log but not play recordings.
- **The recording does not start.** Press play again to get a new link. If it still fails, the file may have been removed.
