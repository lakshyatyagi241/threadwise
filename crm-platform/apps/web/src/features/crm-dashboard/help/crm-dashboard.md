## What it is for

The CRM dashboard is the home of your sales. It answers "how are we doing" for a period you choose, using the deals, contacts, tasks, invoices and payments you are allowed to see.

## How to use it

1. Pick a period: this month, 7, 30 or 90 days, or a custom range. Days are cut in your own time zone.
2. Narrow the numbers with the pipeline, owner and team pickers. The choices stay in the address, so you can share the view.
3. Click a figure or a row to open the records behind it: a funnel stage opens its open deals, an owner opens their deals, a task count opens the task list.

## What each part shows

- **Key figures**: open pipeline and forecast, won value, win rate, average deal size and sales cycle, new contacts and leads, open and overdue tasks, and (when invoicing is on) collected, outstanding and paid invoices. Arrows compare with the previous period of the same length.
- **Pipeline funnel**: deals created in the period that reached each stage, and the share that moved on to the next one.
- **Deals won and lost**: per day, week or month.
- **Owner leaderboard**: owners ranked by value won, with their lost and open deals and logged activities.
- **Lead sources**: leads per source, and the share that became a deal and a won deal.
- **Tasks, receivables aging, recent payments and recent activity.**
- **Revenue report**: the button at the top opens the [revenue report](help:revenue-report), which splits what you invoiced and collected by source, product, owner or team and can be downloaded. It shows when you can read payments and your plan has invoicing.

> You only see numbers from records your role lets you see. A member whose scope is "own" sees only their own deals, contacts, tasks and invoices. A section you have no access to is left out.

## Good to know

- Money is converted into one currency for the totals. A currency with no exchange rate is left out and named.
- The dashboard needs a plan with the sales pipeline. Without it the menu entry is locked.
