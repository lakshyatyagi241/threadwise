## What it is for

A recurring invoice is a schedule that makes a new invoice from a template on dates you set, such as a monthly retainer. The screen lists every schedule with its buyer, how often it repeats, its next and last run, runs, failures, the amount per invoice and its status: **Active**, **Paused** or **Ended**.

## How to use it

1. Select **New schedule**. You can also open an invoice and choose **Make recurring** to start from a copy of it.
2. Under **Schedule**, name it (only you see the name), set **Repeat every** with a unit of days, weeks, months or years, and pick the **First invoice on** date. Optionally set **Stop after** a date, or a **Number of invoices**.
3. Under **What happens to each invoice**, choose **Leave as a draft**, **Issue it** or **Issue and send**. For sending, pick **Send by**: Link, Email or WhatsApp.
4. Under **Invoice template**, copy an existing invoice or build the buyer, lines, tax, discount and notes right there.
5. Read **Next runs** on the side, then select **Create schedule**. From then on it makes invoices by itself.

## Options and settings

- **Tabs and search:** **All**, **Active**, **Paused** and **Ended**. The search box only looks at schedules already loaded, so use **Load more** to search further.
- **Row menu:** **Open**, **Edit**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **Run now** and **Delete**. **Run now** makes the next invoice immediately and the schedule moves on. Deleting stops the schedule; invoices it already made stay in your list.
- A start date in the past makes the missed periods right away, up to 12. A schedule that starts on day 31 runs on the last day of shorter months.
- Periods that fall while a schedule is paused are skipped.
- Editing the rhythm or the template only affects invoices still to come.
- Open a schedule to see its upcoming dates, its template and the **Run history**, one row per period with its result.

## Plan and access

The menu entry is not tied to a plan feature. Viewing, creating, editing and deleting schedules are separate permissions, and buttons you may not use are hidden.

## If something looks wrong

- **A number shows under Failures:** open the schedule and read **Why it failed** in the run history. Fix the template, such as the buyer, the lines or the tax, and save.
- **Email sending fails:** the template needs a buyer email. Add one, or send by link.
- **You cannot edit a schedule:** an ended schedule can no longer be changed. Create a new one.
- **Invoices came out as drafts:** check **What happens to each invoice** on the schedule.
