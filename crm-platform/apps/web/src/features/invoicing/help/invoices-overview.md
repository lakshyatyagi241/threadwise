## What it is for

Invoices lists every invoice in your workspace. Use it to bill customers, share each invoice and see what is still owed. When money is outstanding, a strip above the tabs shows **Outstanding** and **Of which overdue** for each currency. The tabs **All**, **Draft**, **Issued**, **Partially paid**, **Overdue**, **Paid** and **Void** filter the list by status and show how many invoices each holds.

## How to use it

1. Select **New invoice**. Pick a contact or type the buyer's name, set the currency, issue date and payment terms, then add line items with description, quantity, unit price, discount and tax. **Preview** shows the finished page.
2. Select **Save draft** to carry on later, or **Save and issue**. Issuing gives the invoice its next number, locks its lines, prices and taxes, and makes a link available for your customer.
3. In a row's three-dot menu choose **Issue and send** for a draft, or **Share link / mark sent** for an issued invoice. Pick **Share the link myself**, **Email** or **WhatsApp**. Email and WhatsApp need an email address or phone number on the invoice and record the send on the invoice's activity.
4. When money arrives, choose **Record payment**, or **Mark as paid** for the full balance. See [Payments](help:payments-overview).
5. Open an invoice to see its public link, payments, credit notes and activity. On an issued invoice, **Edit details** still changes the contact, due date, notes and similar fields.

## Options and settings

- **Search and filters:** search by number or customer, then narrow by customer, issue date, due date, document type (**Tax invoice**, **Proforma**, **Receipt**), currency or **My invoices**. Sort by **Newest first** or **Oldest first**. **Clear filters** resets everything.
- **Row menu:** the actions that fit the invoice's state and your role, such as **Edit**, **Copy link**, **Duplicate**, **Download PDF**, **Void** and **Delete draft**.
- **Bulk actions:** tick rows to issue drafts, mark links as sent, void or delete drafts. Rows that do not accept the action are skipped.
- **Public link:** on the invoice page you can copy the link, replace it with **New link** (the old one stops working) or **Turn off**.

## Plan and access

The menu entry is not tied to a plan feature. Your role decides what you can do: viewing, creating, editing, issuing, voiding and deleting invoices are separate permissions, and buttons you may not use are hidden. Online payment from the customer's page is set up in [Business profile](help:business-profile-settings).

## If something looks wrong

- **An invoice is missing:** check the status tab, select **Clear filters** and use **Load more**, because the list loads in pages.
- **There is no Issue or Edit option:** only drafts can be issued or edited. For an issued invoice use **Duplicate** to start a corrected draft.
- **There is no Void option:** an invoice with payments needs them refunded first, and one with credit notes needs those voided.
- **"Someone else changed this just now":** reload the page and repeat your change.
