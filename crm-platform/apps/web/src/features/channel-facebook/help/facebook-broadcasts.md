## What it is for

Messenger broadcasts send one message to several people who recently chatted with your Facebook Page. Meta allows a Page to message someone only within 24 hours of their last message, so the list only offers people who can be reached right now.

## How to use it

1. Open **Messenger broadcasts** in the menu. The screen lists your broadcasts with their progress and counts.
2. On the right, under **New broadcast**, enter a **Name** and choose the **Page**. Choosing another Page clears the recipients.
3. Optionally pick a **Template**. It fills the message and sends the template's buttons. Write your own **Message** if you prefer. Use `{name}` where the person's name should go.
4. Tick the people to message, or select **Select all**. The number selected is shown below the list.
5. Select **Create broadcast**. It is saved as a draft.
6. On the draft, select **Start sending**. Sending runs in the background, so it continues if you leave the page.

## Options and settings

- **Pause** stops a broadcast that is sending. Recipients not yet reached stay queued.
- **Retry failed** queues the failed and blocked recipients again. Then press **Start sending**.
- **Delete** removes a broadcast after you confirm.
- Each row shows the counts for sent, failed and blocked, and a progress bar.
- **Inbox** opens the team inbox to see the replies.

## Plan and access

You need the view permission for Messenger broadcasts to open the screen. Creating, starting, pausing, retrying and deleting need the manage permission, and those controls are hidden without it. Connect a Page first in [Facebook Pages](help:facebook-pages).

## If something looks wrong

- **"Connect a Facebook Page first."** No Page is connected yet.
- **No one is listed under recipients.** Nobody has messaged the Page within the last 24 hours. Someone must write to the Page first.
- **Create broadcast stays off.** Enter a name and tick at least one person.
- **Many messages are blocked or failed.** Open the broadcast's counts and retry the failed ones later, when people are reachable again.
- **Setup messages for the Page are missing.** Edit them in [Messenger setup](help:facebook-setup).
