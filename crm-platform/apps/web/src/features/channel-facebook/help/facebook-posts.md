## What it is for

Facebook Posts lists the posts you have created, scheduled or published from this workspace on your connected Facebook Pages. It shows how each one is doing, lets you retry a failed post and keeps your queue in one place.

## How to use it

1. Open **Facebook Posts** in the menu. The page shows the total, scheduled, published and failed counts.
2. Use the status tabs **All**, **Scheduled**, **Published** and **Failed** to narrow the list. The period buttons (**All time**, **7d**, **30d**, **90d**) limit it by date. Type in the search box and press Enter to search by text.
3. Select **Create post** to open the composer. Choose the Page, the post type (photo, album, link, video or reel), the media and the message. Then pick **Publish now** or **Schedule for later**.
4. For a scheduled time, pick a date and time between 10 minutes and 75 days from now, in your own time zone.
5. On a failed or draft post, select the publish icon to send it again now.

> A post that is already live is removed from Facebook as well when you delete it here. This cannot be undone.

## Options and settings

- **Page** narrows the list to one Page. The control appears when you have more than one Page.
- **My posts** shows the newest published posts as a grid, live from Facebook.
- **Insights** opens the Page's reach and engagement figures.
- A failed row shows Facebook's reason under the text. Read it before you retry.

## Plan and access

You need the view permission for Facebook posts to open this screen. Creating, publishing and deleting need the manage permission, and those buttons are hidden without it. Connect your Page first in [Facebook Pages](help:facebook-pages).

## If something looks wrong

- **"Facebook rejected the post."** The reason is shown on the row. Often the media address is not public HTTPS, or the Page was reconnected. Fix it and publish again.
- **The AI tools in the composer are greyed out.** They are not available yet. The composer's photo and video fields take public web addresses. Use the upload button only when storage is set up.
- **No Page is listed.** Connect a Facebook account in Facebook Pages, then come back.
- **A scheduled post has not gone out.** Check that the time is in the future and that the Page still has a live connection.
