## What it is for

Plan checkout is the page you land on when you buy a plan or an add-on pack. It shows what you are buying, lets you add a coupon, collects the billing details that go on your invoice, and starts the payment with the method you choose. To start one, select an upgrade on [Account and plans](help:account-plans).

## How to use it

1. Check the **Order summary** on the right: the plan, the subtotal, any tax and the **Total today**.
2. Choose the **Billing period** (monthly or yearly) and the **Currency** if more than one is offered. The price updates as you change them.
3. If you have a code, type it under **Coupon code** and select **Apply**. The discount appears as its own line. Select **Remove** to take it off.
4. Fill in the **Billing address** block: name, email, company, tax ID, address, city, postal code and country. These are printed on the invoice.
5. Pick a **Payment method**. Only gateways that accept the chosen currency are listed.
6. Select **Pay** with the total shown. You are sent to the provider, shown a card form on the page, or shown the bank transfer instructions, depending on the gateway.
7. After paying you return to [Order history](help:order-history), which shows the result. The plan is applied as soon as the payment is confirmed.

## Options and settings

- **Renew automatically each period** appears only for gateways that support it, and only for paid monthly plans. Leave it off for a single payment. You can cancel a renewal at any time and keep the plan until the end of the paid term.
- **Bank transfer and offline gateways** show the account details and an order reference. Include the reference on the transfer, then upload your receipt as payment proof. The plan activates once the platform team verifies it.
- **Sandbox** next to a gateway means it takes test payments only.

> Moving to a cheaper plan is not available online. Contact support to downgrade.

## Plan and access

Buying needs the permission to buy a plan, add-on or wallet top-up. By default only the Owner role holds it. A plan marked as custom or free cannot be bought here.

## If something looks wrong

- **No payment methods are listed.** No gateway is active for that currency. Pick another currency or ask your platform administrator to activate a gateway.
- **The coupon is rejected.** The code may be expired, used up, or not valid for this plan.
- **You paid but the plan did not change.** Some gateways confirm a few minutes later. Reload [Order history](help:order-history); an order marked pending or awaiting approval is still being confirmed.
