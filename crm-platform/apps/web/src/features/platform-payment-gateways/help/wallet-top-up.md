## What it is for

Wallet top-up is the checkout that adds money to your workspace wallet. The wallet pays for messages your plan does not cover, and for every message if your workspace pays per message. Your balance and its statement are on [Wallet and statement](/billing/wallet).

## How to use it

1. Go to [Wallet and statement](/billing/wallet) and enter a whole amount in **Add money**, or pick one of the quick amounts. The amount must be a whole number of at least 1.
2. Check the total and the currency. Choose a currency if more than one is offered.
3. Fill in the billing details: your name, email and address, and a tax ID if you have one. These appear on the invoice.
4. Choose a payment method. Only gateways that accept the currency are listed.
5. Select **Pay** with the total shown. Depending on the gateway you are sent to the provider, shown a card form, or shown bank transfer instructions.
6. When payment is done you return to [Order history](/billing/orders), which shows the result.

> Opening this page without an amount gives an error. Start from the **Add money** box or a quick amount instead.

## Options and settings

- **Currency**: shown when more than one currency is available.
- **Payment method**: one of the gateways your platform has turned on. A gateway marked Sandbox is a test payment.
- **Billing details**: sent with the order and printed on the invoice.

## Plan and access

Placing an order needs the permission to buy a plan, add-on or wallet top-up. By default only the Owner role holds it. The Settings tile is shown to roles that can view order history, so a role that can view orders but cannot buy sees the checkout and then a message that it may not buy.

## If something looks wrong

- **No payment gateway is configured for the currency.** Ask a platform administrator to turn one on for that currency.
- **The amount is refused.** Use a whole number of at least 1, and enter a larger amount if the gateway has a minimum.
- **The payment window did not open.** Allow pop-ups for this site and try again.
- **The payment failed or is still pending.** Check [Order history](/billing/orders) before paying again.
