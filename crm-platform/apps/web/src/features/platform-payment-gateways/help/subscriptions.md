## What it is for

Subscriptions lists every plan and add-on you pay for with auto-renew through the payment provider. Each card shows what it is, the price and billing cycle, whether it will renew, and the date it is paid until.

## Reading a card

- **Active**: the next charge happens on the date shown.
- **Payment failed**: the latest charge did not go through. The provider keeps retrying and your access continues for the grace period, so update your payment method before it runs out.
- **Cancelled**: it will not renew. You keep access until the paid-until date.

When any payment has failed, a red banner at the top names the subscriptions affected, and the workspace owners also get an in-app notification and an email.

## What was charged

1. Open **Show charges** on a card.
2. Each row is one billing cycle with its date, amount and status. **Open receipt** takes you to the provider's receipt page.
3. If the provider cannot be reached, the table shows your paid orders instead and says so.

## Refresh from provider

**Refresh from provider** asks the provider for the latest state of this subscription and applies it, for example a renewal you have not seen yet. It never charges anything.

## Cancel a subscription

1. Choose **Cancel subscription** on the card. It needs the permission to manage subscriptions.
2. Confirm. The provider is told to stop at the end of the paid cycle.

You keep access until the paid-until date and are not charged again. Buying a new plan ends the old plan's subscription for you, and moving to a cheaper plan stops the old one from renewing, so you are never charged for both.

## If something looks wrong

- A subscription you cancelled still charged: use **Refresh from provider**, then contact support with the order number from Order history.
- A refund is handled by your platform's support team; it goes back to the original payment method.
