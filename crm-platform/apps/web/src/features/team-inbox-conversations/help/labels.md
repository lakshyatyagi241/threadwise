## What it is for

Labels tag conversations so your team can sort, find and filter them, for example Refund, VIP or Follow up. They are the same tags you use on contacts: a label you create here also appears on contacts, and one deleted from either place is gone from both.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Labels**. The list shows every label, its description and how many conversations carry it.
2. Under **New label** type a name, pick a color (a hex color such as `#075E54`, or a color name) and, if you like, a description. Select **Create label**.
3. To change a label, select **Edit**, change the name, color or description and select **Save changes**. Names are unique, ignoring capital letters.
4. In the inbox, open a conversation and select **Label** in its header. Tick the labels that apply. Labels on the conversation show next to its name and in the list.
5. To find conversations, open the **Labels** filter above the list and tick one or more labels. A conversation shows when it carries any of the ticked labels. The filter is kept in the address of the page, so you can reload or share the filtered view.
6. To label many conversations at once, select **Select** above the list, pick the conversations, then use **Label** in the bar to add or remove one label on all of them.

## Options and settings

- **Delete** removes the label from every conversation and every contact. The confirmation tells you how many conversations carry it. This cannot be undone.
- Typing a new name in the header's **Label** menu creates the label and puts it on the conversation, but only for people who may manage labels.

## Plan and access

Putting labels on conversations needs the right to label conversations. Creating, renaming, recoloring and deleting labels needs the right to manage labels. Anyone who can open the inbox can see the labels and filter by them. Labels count toward your plan's limit on tags, shared with contacts.

## If something looks wrong

- **"A label with that name already exists."** Names are unique, ignoring capital letters. Use the existing label.
- **You cannot create another label.** Your plan's tag limit is reached. Delete one you no longer use or ask the workspace owner to upgrade.
- **There is no Label button in a conversation.** You do not have the right to label conversations.
- **Someone else changed the label first.** The list reloaded. Try again.
