## What it is for

Products and services is a price list. You write down what you sell once, with its price, its unit and the tax that usually goes with it, instead of typing the same
details again on every quote and invoice. It is an add-on: the menu entry appears in **Sales** after your workspace has the **Products and services** app.

## Add an item

1. Open **Products and services** and select **Add**.
2. Choose **Product** or **Service**, then give it a **Name**. A **SKU** is optional, and no two items can share one (capital letters do not matter).
3. Pick the **Currency** and type the **Unit price**, for example 1250.50. The number of decimals follows the currency.
4. Optionally write the **Unit** (pcs, kg, hour, month), a **Default tax** in percent and a **Description**.

## Keep the list tidy

- **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** show the item counts. Search by name or SKU, or show only products or only services.
- **Deactivate** keeps an item but stops offering it on new documents; **Activate** brings it back. **Delete** removes it from the list. Documents that were already
  made keep their lines either way.
- If someone else changed an item while you had it open, saving tells you so; close the window and open the item again.

## Who can use it

Viewing, adding, editing and deleting are separate permissions in **Settings > Roles** (they appear once your workspace has the app). The Owner and Admin roles have
them all; the Manager role can view, add and edit.

## Use it on an invoice or estimate

On every line of an invoice, estimate, recurring invoice or sales order, a **Pick from your price list** box appears above the description (only if you may view the
list). Pick an item and the line gets its name, SKU and price, and the tax rate that matches its default tax. Everything stays editable, and a line you type by hand
works exactly as before. Items priced in another currency than the document are listed but cannot be picked.
