## What it is for

The Dashboard is the first page to check each day. It shows how many messages your workspace has sent in a period, how many were delivered, read and answered, and how that compares with the period before. For a deeper look, the Dashboard links to Analytics.

## How to use it

1. Open **Dashboard** from the **Platform** area of the menu, or go to `/dashboard`. A greeting with your name sits at the top, with a one-line summary of the period.
2. Choose the period with the buttons at the top right: **24h**, **7d**, **30d** or **QTD** (quarter to date). The page opens on **7d**. The period is kept in the address of the page, so you can share a link to the same view.
3. Read the four key numbers. **Messages sent** shows the count with a trend against the previous period of the same length, plus how many were delivered and how many failed. **Read rate**, **Deliverability** and **Replies** follow, with the reply rate under **Replies**.
4. Read **Message throughput**. It charts sent, delivered and failed messages by hour for 24h and by day for the longer periods, and names the peak. Under it are the average per hour, the replies, the reply rate and the failed count.
5. Check **Last 20 hours**, a small chart of messages sent per hour, oldest first.
6. Check **Engagement funnel**, which counts the contacts you messaged, how many of them had a message delivered, and how many replied, each as a share of the contacts messaged. Below it, **Conversation activity** shows inbound messages, responding contacts and replies per responding contact, and **Compared with previous period** shows the change in reply rate in percentage points (hidden when the previous period had no messaged contacts). Select **View analytics** to open the full report.

## What the numbers mean

- The trend badges compare the period you picked with the one just before it. A green badge is good news and a red one is not.
- Days and hours follow your workspace timezone, which you can see on the [Account](help:account-profile) page.

## Plan and access

You need the permission to view the dashboard. The Dashboard does not need a particular plan feature, but the Analytics page that **View analytics** opens does.

## If something looks wrong

- **The numbers show a dash.** They are still loading. If a message says the numbers could not be loaded, try again in a moment.
- **The numbers look low.** Check the period. The page opens on 7 days.
- **A message says you do not have access.** Your role does not include the dashboard. Ask a workspace admin.
