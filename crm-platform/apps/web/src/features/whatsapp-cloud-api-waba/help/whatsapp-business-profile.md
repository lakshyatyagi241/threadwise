## What it is for

This is the public profile of each WhatsApp Business number: the picture, the about text, the contact details and the compliance details Meta asks for. Customers see the profile in their WhatsApp chat with your business.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **WhatsApp business profile** under Business and billing.
2. Pick the number. The primary number is shown first.
3. Under **Business Logo**, select **Upload logo** and choose a JPG or PNG picture up to 5 MB. Select the cross to clear a picture you picked but have not saved.
4. Fill in **About**. It is required and can be up to 139 characters. **Description** and **Contact Information** are optional; open them to add the address, email, up to two websites and the industry.
5. Select **Update profile**. A picture you picked is sent with the text, so both are saved together.
6. Under **Compliance Information**, fill in every field and select **Update compliance information**. This is required before you can send catalog messages.

The same profile and compliance form are also on each number's **Health** page.

## Compliance information

- **Legal name of Business** is the registered name of the business.
- **Business Type** is the legal form. For **Partnership** and **Other**, say whether the business is registered. For **Other**, describe the business type.
- **Customer care** and **Grievance officer** each need at least one way to be reached: an email, a mobile number or a landline. Give the grievance officer's name too.
- Phone numbers need the country code, starting with **+**, for example +91 98765 43210.

> Changes are sent to Meta and read back from Meta, so the form shows what Meta holds.

## Plan and access

You need the right to view WhatsApp numbers to open this page. Changing the profile or the compliance information needs the right to manage numbers.

## If something looks wrong

- **The profile cannot be read.** Meta did not answer. Try again in a few minutes, or check the number on its **Health** page.
- **The picture is refused.** Use a JPG or PNG up to 5 MB. Meta does not take other formats for the profile picture.
- **Meta rejects the compliance details.** The message from Meta is shown next to the form. Correct the field it names; the form does not change your other answers.
- **The number is missing from the list.** Connect it first in [WhatsApp numbers](help:whatsapp-numbers-overview).
