## What it is for

Saved customers are people whose name, company and delivery address you have stored in advance. When one of them orders on WhatsApp, their details are shown and they only need to reply YES to confirm.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Saved customers** under Business.
2. Select **Add a customer** in the form at the top. Enter the phone number with its country code, the name, an optional company and the full delivery address.
3. Select **Save customer**. The customer appears in the list below.
4. To change one, select **Edit** on its row. The form fills in, and **Save customer** updates it. **Clear** empties the form.
5. To search, type a name, phone number or company in the search box.
6. To remove a customer, select **Delete** and confirm. Their saved details are deleted, and their past orders stay in the order list.

## Options and settings

There are no settings on this page. Each customer has a phone number, a name, an optional company and one delivery address.

## Plan and access

You need the right to view saved customers to open this page, and the right to change them to add, edit or delete. Without the change right the list is read-only.

## If something looks wrong

- **The phone number is refused.** Enter it with the country code, and digits only after it.
- **A customer's address does not fill in on an order.** Check that the phone number matches the number the customer orders from, including the country code.
- **The list does not load.** Select **Try again**.
