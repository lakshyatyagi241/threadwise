## What it is for

Proposals and Estimates are two lists that work the same way. A proposal pitches scope and price; an estimate quotes a price before you invoice. Each document goes from **Draft** to **Sent**, then **Viewed** when the buyer opens it, and ends as **Accepted**, **Declined**, **Expired** or **Converted**. The tabs show a count for each status, and **Open value** adds up documents that are sent, viewed or accepted.

## How to use it

1. Select **New proposal** or **New estimate**. Add the buyer (a contact, or a typed name, email and phone), a title, an optional deal, the currency and **Valid until**. The buttons **+7 days**, **+14 days** and **+30 days** fill the date; **No expiry** clears it.
2. Add line items with description, quantity, unit price, discount and tax. You can also set a default tax rate and a discount on the whole document, and add notes and terms, starting from a template if you like.
3. Select **Save draft**. The number is assigned when you first save.
4. Select **Send** and choose **Link**, **Email** or **WhatsApp**. The link lets the buyer view the document and accept it with a signature or decline it. **Email** sends your message with the link. On the WhatsApp tab, **Open in WhatsApp** prepares the message for you to send yourself, then use **I shared it: mark as sent** on the Link tab.
5. When a document is accepted, use **Convert to invoice**, **Create sales order** or **Create a deal** from its menu. Converting locks the document and cannot be undone.

## Options and settings

- **Filters:** search by number, title or buyer; filter by owner (including **Mine**), contact, deal, or **Valid until** (**Expires within 7 days**, **Date has passed**).
- **Row menu:** **Open**, **Edit** (drafts), **Send**, **Copy public link**, **Mark as sent**, **Record as accepted** or **Record as declined** (when the buyer answered by phone or on paper), **Revise (back to draft)**, **Extend validity**, **Duplicate**, **Print / PDF**, **Activity** and **Delete**.
- **Bulk delete:** tick rows and select **Delete**. Accepted and converted documents are kept.
- **Keyboard:** press `?` on the list to see the shortcuts.

## Plan and access

The menu entries are not tied to a plan feature. You need permission to view proposals and estimates. Creating, editing, sending, converting and deleting are separate permissions, and **Create sales order** also needs permission to create sales orders.

## If something looks wrong

- **You cannot edit a document:** only drafts can be edited. Use **Revise** to take a sent one back to a draft.
- **Send says to add the buyer's email or phone:** add it in the buyer details, then send.
- **The buyer cannot open the link:** the link opens once the document is sent. A revoked or expired link needs **Make a new link** on the Link tab.
- **The document shows Expired:** use **Extend validity** with a new date.
