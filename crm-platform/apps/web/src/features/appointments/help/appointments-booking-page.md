## What it is for

The booking page is the page your customers open to choose a time and book. Out of the box it looks the same for every business. Booking page appearance lets you make it look like yours: your logo, your colours, a welcome message, the wording of the final button, a font and the shape of the corners. A booking type can have its own look on top of that. The same look is used on the page where a customer manages or moves their booking.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Booking page appearance**. You can also open it from the **Booking page appearance** card on **Appointment settings**.
2. Under **Logo and cover image**, upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF. The logo can be up to 1 MB and the cover image up to 3 MB. Switch on **Use my Branding logo and colours when empty** to take anything you leave empty from **Settings**, **Branding**.
3. Under **Colours and background**, choose the **Primary colour** and, if you want a different one, the **Button colour**. Type a colour like #1a73e8 or use the colour picker. Choose the **Page background**: a solid colour, a soft tint of your primary colour, or your cover image as a banner.
4. Under **Wording**, write the **Page title**, a **Welcome message** shown above the booking form, and the **Confirm button label**.
5. Under **Fonts and corners**, pick a font, square, rounded or pill corners, and light, dark or follow the customer's device.
6. Under **Page options**, choose whether to show the language switch and the "Powered by" line.
7. Check the **Live preview** on the right. It is the real booking page with sample times. Switch between **Desktop** and **Mobile**, between the **Booking page** and the **Service list**, between light and dark, and between left-to-right and right-to-left. Nothing in the preview is booked.
8. Select **Save appearance**. The page address under **Page address** has a **Copy** button and an **Open page** link; the change shows there as soon as you save.

## A different look for one booking type

1. Open the booking type and select **Page appearance** at the top, or open **Page appearance** from the menu of the booking type in the list.
2. Set a logo, cover image, colours, welcome message or button label. Anything you leave empty uses the workspace look, and the form tells you what it will use.
3. Select **Save appearance**. To go back to the workspace look, select **Use workspace appearance**. This removes everything set for that booking type, including its pictures.

## Readability

Text colours are chosen for you. Text on buttons is black or white, whichever reads better on your button colour. Where your primary colour is used as text or as a focus ring, it is made darker or lighter just enough to be readable, on both the light and the dark page. The editor warns you when your button colour is hard to tell apart from the page, or when your primary colour is too pale to read as text and will be adjusted.

## Options and settings

- The font choices are fonts already on the customer's device, so the page loads quickly.
- **Follow the device** shows the dark page to customers whose phone or computer is set to dark.
- Only plain text is accepted for wording. Colours must be in the form #RRGGBB. A picture is always one you uploaded; you cannot point the page at an address.
- A booking type can set its own logo, cover image, colours, welcome message and button label. The font, corners, light or dark and the footer are always the workspace's.

## Plan and access

You need permission to view booking types to see the screen, and permission to edit booking types to change it. Removing "Powered by" is part of white-label branding. On a plan without it the switch is locked and the line stays on. If your plan loses white-label branding later, the line comes back by itself.

## If something looks wrong

- **Save says the appearance changed elsewhere.** Select **Reload latest**. Your unsaved edits are replaced.
- **A picture is not accepted.** Use a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF within the size limit. SVG files are not accepted.
- **The cover image background shows nothing.** Upload a cover image first; without one the page uses a solid background.
- **A change from Settings, Branding takes a moment to show.** The booking page keeps your look for up to two minutes. Saving the appearance shows it at once.
- Using your own web address, and placing the booking page inside another website, are not available yet.
