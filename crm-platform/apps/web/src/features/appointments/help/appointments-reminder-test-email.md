## What it is for

A test email sends one reminder step to you with made-up details, so you can read the subject and the text as the customer will. The subject starts with **[Test]**. Only email can be tested: there is no WhatsApp test, because a WhatsApp template message to a real number costs money and needs that person's consent.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Appointment settings**, then **Reminders and follow-ups**, and open the booking type.
2. Save your changes first. The test sends the **saved** step, so a step you just added, or edits you have not saved, ask you to save.
3. Open the step. Under it, leave **Send the test to** empty to send to your own email address, or type another address, for example a colleague's.
4. Select **Send a test email**. The answer says where it went, with part of the address hidden. The result also arrives in your notifications a moment later.

## Limits

- The step needs email as one of its channels. A WhatsApp-only step has no test, and a person who may not edit booking types does not see the control.
- At most 10 test emails an hour for the whole workspace. Past that the answer says to try again later.
- Quiet hours and the customer consent check do not apply to a test. Your workspace's choice to use email for appointment messages does.
- If your user has no email address and you leave the field empty, nothing is sent and the screen says so.

## If something looks wrong

- **Nothing arrives.** Check the spam folder, and that email is switched on for appointment messages under Message channels and templates. The notification tells you if the send failed.
- **The button is greyed out.** The step is new or has unsaved changes. Save the reminders first.
