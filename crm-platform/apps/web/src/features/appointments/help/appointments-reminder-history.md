## What it is for

Each appointment keeps a record of its reminders, so you can answer "did they get it, and why not?" without guessing.

## How to read it

1. Open the appointment from **Appointments**. The **Reminders** section lists every step: the confirmation, the reminders, the follow-up and so on, with its time and a status such as **Scheduled**, **Waiting**, **Sent**, **Not sent** or **Failed**.
2. Under a step you may see:
   - **Sent by Email because the first channel could not deliver it**: the first channel was skipped or failed, and the next one sent it.
   - **Channels tried, in order**: each channel with what became of it, and the reason when it was not used, for example no recorded consent or a WhatsApp message that is not approved yet.
   - What WhatsApp reported afterwards: delivered, failed or not delivered in time. Email has no delivery report.
   - **Replies with the buttons**: which of Confirm, Reschedule and Cancel the customer tapped, and when.

The history shows codes turned into sentences. It never holds the customer's number, address or the message text.

## Who sees it

Anyone who may open the appointment. A person who may only see their own appointments sees the history of those.

## If something looks wrong

- **A step says Scheduled but nothing arrives.** It is not due yet. Quiet hours or a channel that is not connected can also hold it; the status then says **Waiting**.
- **The button replies are missing.** The customer has not tapped a button, or the step was sent without buttons.
