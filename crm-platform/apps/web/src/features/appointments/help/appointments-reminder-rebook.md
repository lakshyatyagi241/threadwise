## What it is for

A rebook message goes to the contact a set number of days after an appointment you marked completed, asking them to book again. A dentist might send it after six months, a salon after five weeks.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Appointment settings**, then **Reminders and follow-ups**, and open the booking type.
2. In the timeline select **Add step**, then **Rebook message, some days later**.
3. Set the number of days after the appointment (30 by default, up to a year), the channels and the message.
4. Switch the step **On** and save. A new step starts off.

## Good to know

- It is not sent if the contact has already booked again, or if the appointment was not completed.
- It counts as a marketing message, so your workspace's marketing consent rules apply. A contact who opted out is skipped.
- A ready-made sequence includes a rebook step, saved switched off so you can set the days first.
- The appointment's history and the reminder report show when a rebook message was skipped and why.
- Rebook messages are part of the Appointments upgrade tier. See [Reminder buttons and rebook messages on your plan](help:appointment-reminder-plans).

## If something looks wrong

- **It was not sent.** Check that the step is On, the appointment is completed, the contact has no later booking and consent allows marketing.
