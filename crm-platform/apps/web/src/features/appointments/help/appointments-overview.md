## What it is for

Appointments lists every booking your customers made, from chat, from your booking pages or added by your team. Each booking has a status: pending, confirmed, completed, no-show, cancelled or declined. Use this screen to confirm requests, mark what happened and move a booking when plans change.

## How to use it

1. Open **Appointments** in the menu. The **Upcoming** tab shows pending and confirmed bookings. The **Past** tab shows everything that has already started.
2. Pick a view with **View**: **List**, **Week** or **Month**. In a calendar view, use the arrows and **Today** to move through dates.
3. Select a booking's row to open its detail panel. It shows the time, the customer, answers to the booking questions, reminders and the manage link.
4. Use the row's **More actions** menu, or the buttons in the panel, for the action you need: **Confirm**, **Decline**, **Mark completed**, **Mark no-show**, **Reschedule**, **Copy manage link** or **Cancel**.
5. Select **Your bookings** actions such as **New booking type** in the top bar to add a service. Press **/** to jump to the search box.

> Confirm and decline ask first only for requests you refuse. Cancelling asks for confirmation, and the customer is told.

## Options and settings

- **Search** matches the customer, phone, email or service.
- **Status** and **Booking type** narrow the list. **Clear** removes them.
- The tab counts follow the search and type, not the status.
- The list loads more bookings at the bottom. A calendar shows the first bookings of the range if there are very many.
- **Booking types**, **Settings** and **New booking type** in the top bar open the set-up screens.
- **Calendar out of sync.** When Google Calendar is connected, the app looks about once an hour at the Google event of each confirmed booking in the next 60 days. If the event was deleted, cannot be found, was moved or had its guests changed in Google, the booking shows **Calendar out of sync** with the reason, and the person who owns it gets one notification. The booking itself is never changed on its own. With the update permission you choose **Push our time back to Google** (the event is written again from the booking) or **Accept Google's change** (a moved event moves the booking and a deleted event cancels it, with the usual messages to the customer, after you confirm).

## Plan and access

You need permission to view appointments. Confirming, moving, completing and cancelling need the update permission, and the buttons are hidden without it. Appointment booking is a plan feature. When your plan does not include it, bookings cannot be created or changed. Your plan can also limit how many bookings you take each month.

## If something looks wrong

- **No bookings appear.** Check the tab and the filters. A booking with a past start time is under **Past**.
- **Mark no-show is missing.** It appears only after a confirmed booking's start time has passed.
- **Calendar out of sync stays after you pushed.** Google may have refused the change; the message says why. Check that Google Calendar is connected in [Appointment settings](help:appointments-settings).
- **"That time was just taken" or a conflict message.** Someone else changed the booking. Reload to see the latest version.
- Booking types and hours are set up in [Booking types](help:appointments-booking-types) and [Appointment settings](help:appointments-settings).
