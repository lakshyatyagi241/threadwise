## What it is for

A booking type is one service customers can book, such as a 30-minute demo or an in-person fitting. Each one has its own length, weekly hours, date overrides, price, questions and reminders. Customers book it from a chat flow or from its public booking page.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Booking types**. You can also open **Booking types** from **Appointments**.
2. Select **New booking type** to open the editor, or open an existing service from its name.
3. Work through the five steps shown at the top: **Service** (name, description, location, colour and public link), **Timing** (duration, start step, capacity, buffers, notice and how far ahead people can book), **Availability** (weekly hours and date overrides), **Pricing** and **Messaging** (reminders and follow-ups, questions and the four customer messages).
4. Select **Create booking type** or **Save changes**. A new type starts paused, so you can check it first.
5. Turn the **Active** switch on in the list when you are ready. A paused service takes no new bookings.

> Pricing records the fee, tax and deposit with each booking. Online payment is not collected yet, so nothing is charged.

## Options and settings

- In the list, **Copy public link** copies the booking page address. It works only while the type is active.
- The row menu offers **Edit**, **Copy public link**, **Open booking page**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.
- **Delete** removes the booking type. Existing bookings stay in your list.
- The editor warns you about unsaved changes when you try to leave.

## Plan and access

Viewing booking types needs the booking type read permission. Creating, editing, pausing and duplicating need the create or update permission, and deleting needs the delete permission. The Google Calendar step appears only when a calendar is set up for your workspace. See [Appointment settings](help:appointments-settings).

## If something looks wrong

- **Save is blocked.** A highlighted field needs fixing. The top of the form lists how many fields need attention.
- **Customers see no times.** Check that the type is active, its weekly hours are set and it has a time zone. The **Slot preview** on the right shows the bookable times.
- **Booking page says the service is paused.** Turn the **Active** switch back on in the list.
- **"That did not work" on a switch.** The change was undone. Try again, or reload to see the current state.
