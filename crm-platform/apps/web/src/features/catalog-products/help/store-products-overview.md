## What it is for

Products is the list of what you sell. Each product has a name, optional SKU, price, stock, images and a visibility status. The same products feed your storefront and the WhatsApp catalog. The table shows image, name, SKU, price and whether the product is in stock.

## How to use it

1. Select **Add product**. In **Basics**, enter a **Name** and, if you like, a SKU, a short description (shown on catalog cards and WhatsApp share links) and a full description.
2. In **Media**, paste a direct image URL as the primary image, and add more image URLs to the gallery. Square images of 1024 by 1024 pixels or larger work best.
3. In **Pricing**, enter the **Price**, an optional **Compare-at price** (shown struck through) and the **Currency**. A profit indicator shows the percentage off.
4. In **Inventory & shipping**, set **Stock qty** (blank means unlimited), **Weight**, **Available for sale** and **Sort order** (lower numbers first).
5. Under **Visibility**, choose **Active** (live on the storefront), **Draft** (hidden, editable) or **Archived** (hidden, kept in records). Add a **Category** and **Tags** if you want, check the **Storefront preview**, then select **Create product**.

## Options and settings

- **Search** by name or SKU. The list has numbered pages of 25.
- **Share** opens the storefront link and the WhatsApp link to copy. You can also type a WhatsApp number with country code and send the product to that customer from a connected sender.
- **Edit** reopens the form. **Delete** removes the product here; it stays on Meta until you remove it there.
- Unticking **Available for sale** marks a product sold out while keeping it visible.

## Plan and access

You need permission to view products to open this screen. Adding, editing and deleting each need their own permission, and sending from **Share** needs the catalog send permission. Unlike the WhatsApp catalog, this screen does not depend on a plan feature.

## If something looks wrong

- **A product is not found:** the search matches name or SKU only. Select **Clear search**.
- **An image will not save:** use a full address starting with https://.
- **A product does not show on the storefront:** its visibility must be **Active**.
- **Share shows a note instead of a link:** the storefront link needs your storefront slug set up first, and the WhatsApp link needs a connected phone number.
