## What it is for

Reviews is where you moderate what customers write about your products. A review shows on the storefront product page only after you approve it. Reviews are grouped in three tabs: **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**, each with a count.

## How to use it

1. Open **Reviews**. New reviews wait in **Pending**.
2. Read each card. It shows the star rating out of 5, the customer's name (or **Anonymous**), the product, how long ago it was written and the review text.
3. Select **Approve** to publish it on the product page, or **Reject** to keep it hidden.
4. To remove a review for good, select **Delete** and confirm. This cannot be undone.
5. Switch tabs to look back. An approved review can be rejected later, and a rejected one approved.

## Options and settings

- **Tabs:** the buttons on a card depend on the tab. **Pending** offers Approve, Reject and Delete; **Approved** offers Reject and Delete; **Rejected** offers Approve and Delete.
- **Pages:** the list is split into numbered pages of 30 reviews.
- A review of a product that was deleted shows **a deleted product** instead of its name.

## Plan and access

You need permission to view reviews to open this screen. Approving, rejecting and deleting need the moderation permission; without it you can read reviews but the buttons are hidden. This screen does not depend on a plan feature.

## If something looks wrong

- **A tab says there are no reviews:** nothing is waiting there. Check the other tabs, because the counts show where reviews sit.
- **A review does not appear on the product page:** check it is in **Approved**. Pending and rejected reviews are never shown to customers.
- **The buttons do nothing or are missing:** your role may lack the moderation permission. Ask a workspace admin.
- **The list could not be loaded:** select **Try again**.
